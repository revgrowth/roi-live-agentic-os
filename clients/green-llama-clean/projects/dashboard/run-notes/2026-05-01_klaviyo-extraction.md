6. **Customer Thank You flow at 59.15% open rate** — strong cohort hygiene signal.
7. **Email Marketing Exclusions segment = 2,945** (about 4% of total recipients). Suppression hygiene is reasonable.
8. **Klaviyo-attributed revenue $6,149.68 in April vs Shopify total sales $13,654** = ~45% Klaviyo attribution. Healthy attribution coverage for a brand that isn't email-led.
