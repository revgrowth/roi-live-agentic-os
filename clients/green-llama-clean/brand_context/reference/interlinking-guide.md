## KEY POINTS FOR ADDING CROSS-LINKS

### WHERE to add them:
- **Right after the Verdict section** (before the FAQ)
- Create a new paragraph or callout box
- Use a transitional phrase like "Want to see more comparisons?" or "Ready to compare more options?"

### HOW to format them:
- Use a short intro line (1 sentence)
- List the 3 OTHER articles (not the current one)
- Use bullet points with links
- Keep it clean and scannable

### WHAT to link:
- Link to the OTHER 3 Priority 2 articles
- Do NOT link to the current article (that would be circular)
- Example: In Article 1, link to Articles 2, 3, and 4

### PLACEHOLDER URLS:
Replace these with your actual blog URLs once published:
- INSERT_ARTICLE_1_URL → https://greenllamaclean.com/blogs/news/green-llama-vs-seventh-generation
- INSERT_ARTICLE_2_URL → https://greenllamaclean.com/blogs/news/green-llama-vs-dropps
- INSERT_ARTICLE_3_URL → https://greenllamaclean.com/blogs/news/green-llama-vs-blueland
- INSERT_ARTICLE_4_URL → https://greenllamaclean.com/blogs/news/green-llama-vs-earth-breeze

## STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

1. **Open your article** in your Shopify blog editor
2. **Find the Verdict section** (near the end, before FAQ)
3. **Position cursor** at the end of the Verdict paragraph
4. **Press Enter** to create a new paragraph
5. **Type your intro line:** "Want to compare more options?" or similar
6. **Add bullet points** with links to the other 3 articles
7. **Replace INSERT_ARTICLE_X_URL** with your actual URLs once published
8. **Save and publish**

---

## TIMING NOTE

You can add these cross-links in two ways:

**Option A: Before Publishing**
- Add the placeholder links now (INSERT_ARTICLE_1_URL, etc.)
- Once all 4 articles are published, go back and replace with real URLs

**Option B: After Publishing**
- Publish articles without cross-links first
- Once all 4 are live, go back and add the real URLs

Either way works fine. Option A is slightly more efficient if you're publishing all 4 at once.
