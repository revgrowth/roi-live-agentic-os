## Voice positioning

Green Llama writes as a science-led, intellectually honest brand that takes the precautionary approach to chemical exposure without using fear to sell. The voice respects the reader's intelligence, names limitations of evidence, and earns trust through verifiable sources rather than rhetorical flourishes. Non-toxic is the formulation principle, not the marketing claim.

## Dominant tonal qualities

- **Informed and honest** — share what the science says, including its limitations
- **Accessible** — explain chemistry and toxicology in plain language at Grade 6–8 reading level without losing accuracy
- **Empowering, not fear-based** — help readers make informed choices; state risks proportionally
- **Precise** — use correct scientific and regulatory language; correct mistakes openly
- **Direct** — state facts; trust the reader; do not hide behind vague language

## What the voice does NOT do

- No fear-mongering language or scare tactics
- No absolute safety claims ("completely safe for everyone")
- No contested science presented as settled consensus
- No claims attributed to organizations that did not make them
- No dramatic one-word fragments, binary contrasts, or pull-quote-ready sentences
- No adverbs (-ly intensifiers), softeners, throat-clearing openers, em-dashes, or passive voice

## Hard rules from the GLC overlay

- **Pronoun:** "we" is permitted in body copy (DTC override per Core Standards §3.4). Kay and Matt are named explicitly at attribution moments.
- **Em-dashes:** banned anywhere in editorial content. Use spaced en-dash or restructure. Also an AI-detection countermeasure since LLMs over-produce em-dashes.
- **Llaina mascot:** out of scope for blog editorial. Stays in social, packaging, and brand-campaign territory.
- **Three-item lists:** factual enumerations permitted (acidic/alkaline/neutral; proteases/amylases/lipases). Rhetorical pattern-of-three banned ("Purpose. Clarity. Impact.").
- **Voice Charter v5.0** at [reference/voice-charter-v5.md](./reference/voice-charter-v5.md) is NOT active for blog editorial. It applies to other channels only.
