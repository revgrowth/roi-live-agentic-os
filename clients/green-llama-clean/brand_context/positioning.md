# Green Llama Clean — Positioning

## Brand definition

Green Llama Clean makes home cleaning products formulated without ingredients known or suspected to cause harm to human health. The product line is powder, tablets, and solid bars. No liquids. Packaging is compostable. The brand is certified Women-Owned, Leaping Bunny certified across the line, and EWG Verified on the Dishwasher Tablets, Dishwasher Powder, and the new Laundry Powder formula.

## Mission

Create cleaning products with ingredients not harmful to health or the environment. Give consumers honest, science-backed information to make informed choices. Make non-toxic the baseline, not the premium.

## Primary positioning angle

**Science-led non-toxic cleaning, formulated by a chemist.** Green Llama's edge is the founding pair: Kay Baker (occupational therapist, child-safety lens) and Matthew Keasey (molecular neuroscientist, ingredient science lens). Every product decision is evaluated against the EWG and EPA Safer Choice ingredient databases. Every blog article is reviewed for scientific accuracy by a Ph.D. The brand is built around radical transparency: every ingredient disclosed, every claim verifiable.

## What Green Llama stands for

Six core values from the GL Blog Writing Guidelines:

1. **Radical Transparency** — every ingredient disclosed. No proprietary blends. No hidden fragrance chemicals.
2. **Science-Based Formulation** — ingredient decisions driven by toxicology and chemistry, not marketing trends. Full safety profile evaluation, including chronic exposure and environmental persistence.
3. **Health First** — products designed to protect the most vulnerable household members (children, infants, pregnant women, pets). Precautionary approach: when evidence indicates a risk, GL excludes the ingredient even when regulation does not yet require exclusion.
4. **Environmental Responsibility** — compostable packaging, concentrated formats, reduced plastic waste, lower shipping carbon.
5. **Accessibility** — non-toxic at conventional cost-per-use rather than premium pricing.
6. **Intellectual Honesty** — never overstate the science, never attribute claims to organizations that did not make them, never use fear-based marketing beyond what evidence supports.

## Competitive landscape (high-level)

Per the topical map and future-clusters reference, primary competitors in the eco-clean category:

- **Branch Basics** — concentrate model. GLC differentiator: solid/tablet format, more rigorous certification on flagship products, broader product line.
- **Blueland** — refill tablet model, similar format. GLC differentiators: founder-scientist credibility, women-owned certification, more comprehensive EWG verification on flagship products.
- **Earth Breeze** — laundry sheets. GLC differentiator: powder format avoids the PVA controversy entirely; concentrated dosing without dissolution-residue concerns.
- **Conventional cleaning brands** (Tide, Mrs. Meyer's, Method) — GLC differentiator: actual ingredient transparency vs. proprietary fragrance blends; no synthetic VOCs, 1,4-dioxane, optical brighteners, or phthalates.

## Ingredients GLC products never contain

Non-exhaustive list, drawn directly from GL_Blog_Content_SOP_v1.1: synthetic VOCs, petroleum-based ingredients, 1,4-dioxane, triclosan, chlorine bleach, ammonia, phthalates, synthetic fragrance, optical brighteners, preservatives, dyes.

Scented options use natural essential oil blends only. GLC never uses synthetic fragrance.

## What we don't claim

Per the agency Citation Discipline SOP and the GL Blog Content SOP, GLC content does not:

- Claim a chemical "will definitely harm you" when evidence shows association, not causation
- Present contested science (e.g., the PVA biodegradability debate) as settled
- Cite a study without a verifiable URL or DOI
- Apply occupational exposure data to household-use claims, or animal-study data to human-effect claims, without explicit disclosure
- Use fear or guilt as emotional triggers in editorial content (Voice Charter v5.0's emotional-targeting framework does not apply to blog editorial — see [sops/GL_Editorial_Overlay_v1.md](./sops/GL_Editorial_Overlay_v1.md) ruling D1)
