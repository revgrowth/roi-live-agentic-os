# ICP — Yellow Jacket Oil Tools

**Last updated:** April 28, 2026
**Note:** This ICP is derived from the handoff document and kickoff materials. Validate against Sales Lead and Kelly recordings once received — especially customer language and objection patterns.

---

## Primary Audience

**Completion engineers and procurement leads at oil and gas operators.**

These are technical buyers who know the product category. They do not need frac plugs or perforating guns explained from scratch — they need a reason to trust Yellow Jacket's specs, evaluate performance data, and compare against what they're already running. Content should treat them as professionals making high-stakes operational decisions, not as learners being introduced to a concept.

Secondary audience: engineering managers and operations directors who influence or approve vendor selection.

---

## How They Buy

- The sales cycle involves multiple stakeholders: engineering evaluates technical fit, procurement handles commercial terms, legal reviews IP-adjacent content and competitor claims.
- Customers often arrive with existing vendor relationships (WellBoss, Repeat Precision). The evaluation trigger is typically a performance gap, a cost driver, or a basin-specific challenge their current vendor hasn't addressed.
- AI-sourced answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) are increasingly part of how engineers begin vendor research. This is the core AEO opportunity.

---

## How They Ask Questions

Customer language is different from internal product language. The Sales Lead recording is the primary source for this data. Until that recording is received, use these signals from the handoff:

- They search for completion outcomes (plug-and-perf operations, stage isolation, run success rates) before they search for brand names.
- They search by basin (Permian completion tools, Eagle Ford frac plug, Bakken perforating guns).
- They search by decision frame (composite vs. dissolvable frac plug, wireline vs. TCP perforating).
- Common support-email and phone language will be captured in the Sales Lead recording. Populate this section once received.

## Content Tone by Audience

| Audience | What They Need From Content | What to Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Completion engineers | Specific data, technical comparison, failure mode analysis | Oversimplification, vague claims, marketing language |
| Procurement leads | Cost comparison, vendor reliability signals, contractual clarity | Pure technical jargon without operational framing |
| Engineering managers | Performance outcomes, risk reduction, basin-specific case data | Either extreme — needs to bridge technical and business |

## Competitive Evaluation Triggers

From the handoff, customers evaluate Yellow Jacket against:
- **WellBoss** — most direct frac plug competitor
- **Repeat Precision** — frac plug competitor with growing AI visibility (urgency signal for this engagement)
- **DynaEnergetics** — perforating gun competitor with decent AI visibility
- **Hunting Titan** — global-scale competitor; evaluation usually comes down to precision vs. procurement simplicity

Win/loss stories from the Sales Lead recording will ground this section with real decision-driver data. Populate once received.
