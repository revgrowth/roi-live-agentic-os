## Brand Voice Framework (Pre-Recording)

The following is derived from the handoff document and kickoff materials. Treat as provisional until recordings validate or override.

### Tone Signals

- **Technical but not academic.** The audience are completion engineers and procurement leads who know what they're doing. Write at their level, not above or below it.
- **Specific over vague.** Run counts. Pressure ratings. Success rates. Dissolution rates. If a number exists and is cleared for publication, use it.
- **Earned confidence.** Yellow Jacket operates in a market with much larger competitors (Hunting Titan, DynaEnergetics). The voice should reflect real performance data and customer results — not bravado.
- **Basin-aware.** Permian. Eagle Ford. Bakken. Customers think regionally. Content should too.

### What Yellow Jacket Is Not

- Not a global giant. Not trading on scale. Trading on precision, performance, and relationship.
- Not a generalist. Built specifically around frac plugs and perforating gun systems — depth of focus is a feature.

### The Core Claim (Provisional)

To be validated against recordings. Current working frame: Yellow Jacket does both frac plugs and perforating guns at a depth that larger competitors who do everything cannot match. The M1NT line (composite, dissolvable, hybrid variants) and the perforating gun systems together represent a completions toolkit built for engineers who need the specifics, not the pitch.
