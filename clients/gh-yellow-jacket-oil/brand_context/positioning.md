# Positioning — Yellow Jacket Oil Tools

**Last updated:** April 28, 2026

---

## The Core Position

Yellow Jacket Oil Tools manufactures frac plugs and perforating gun systems for oil and gas completion operations. The positioning advantage is depth of focus: Yellow Jacket builds specifically for this toolkit, while larger competitors (Hunting Titan, DynaEnergetics at scale) treat it as one product line among many.

The M1NT frac plug line (composite, dissolvable, and hybrid variants) combined with the perforating gun systems gives completion engineers a paired solution from a manufacturer who can answer technical questions without routing through three internal departments.

This is the working position. It requires validation against Jimmy's and the Sales Lead's recordings before being used in published content.
