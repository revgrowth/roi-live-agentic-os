## Content Quality Standards (Client-Specific)

In addition to Core Standards §8 (Stop Slop rules):

- **Specific numbers over vague claims.** AI systems cite specifics. Capture publishable metrics: run counts, pressure ratings, success rates, dissolution rates. Do not generalize.
- **Customer language over internal language.** How customers ask questions (support emails, phone calls, ChatGPT-style queries) takes priority over how G&H describes products internally. Verify this from recording data.
- **Basin-specific context.** Permian, Eagle Ford, Bakken, and other relevant basins should appear in content where applicable. No competitor is doing this — it's a differentiator.
- **Named authors and expert bios.** E-E-A-T signals require real people behind content. Engineer, sales lead, and/or Kelly can serve as authors depending on topic.
- **Yellow Jacket voice, not generic industry content.** If it could have been written without the recordings, it wasn't written well enough.
