# ALVARA Positioning

Source: Section 6 (Strategic Foundation) and Section 3 (Product, Brand, Audience, Competitive Set) of `inputs/alvara-context-dump.md`.

---

## Positioning principle

ROI.live exceeds industry benchmarks across every client and vertical. The April 4 Growth Partnership Proposal states this as a position; the engagement carries the claim through every deliverable.

---

## Whitespace

MUDWTR went young and playful. AG1 went broad and mainstream. Four Sigmatic went product-wide and approachable. Nobody owns the mature, nature-rooted, ceremonial-grade position that ALVARA occupies. The 45-65 premium wellness audience plus ancient-medicine-meets-Stanford-science-meets-regenerative-beekeeping is unclaimed.

---

## Audience

45-65, split gender, premium buyer, mature wellness audience. Sub-segments map to the ten campaign angles below; the full segment-by-segment treatment lives in `icp.md`.

## Engagement identity (Section 12 of the context dump)

This is a DTC subscription growth partnership. Content is one of several acquisition channels, not the whole job. The shape of work resembles French Broad Chocolates (protecting and documenting voice that already exists) more than Coastal Air (building voice and content from scratch). The math has subscription complexity East Perry did not have. The regulatory weight is closer to a pharmaceutical brand than a typical CPG.
