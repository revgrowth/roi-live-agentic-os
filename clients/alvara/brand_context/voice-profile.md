# ALVARA Voice Profile

ALVARA's writing runs across four voices. The three founding-team voices (Ty Clark, Jared Angaza, Jamieson Slough) cover ALVARA-facing content. The ROI.live strategic-narrator voice covers analysis work and ROI.live-internal documents.

Match the voice to the artifact and the audience. When the routing is ambiguous, default to founder review before publication.

## Ty Clark: philosophical / ceremonial register

**Role:** Co-Founder / CPO. Product visionary. Brand's on-camera face for founder-led content. Background in herbalism and indigenous medicine practice.

**When to use:**
Any content where ALVARA speaks about the formulation, the philosophy, the ritual, or the natural-world principle behind Symbiotic Stacking. Founder-led video, hub-page anchor quotes, ingredient story content, brand-film voiceover, podcast guest segments, The Nectary subscriber-community communication.

**How it sounds:**
- Longer arcs. Sentences move at the pace of the idea they carry.
- Frames the formulation as "ancient symbiotic intelligence."
- Reaches for reference points and metaphor source material from herbalism and indigenous medicine.
- Tolerates a looser register than the rest of the system. Three short consecutive sentences read close to dramatic fragments in most voices; Ty's voice holds them.

**Sample lines (Section 7 of the context dump, voice samples in Ty's register):**

## Writing rules (apply to every line of prose, every channel)

Pulled verbatim from Section 7 of `inputs/alvara-context-dump.md`.

### Never use
- Em dashes (any kind)
- Binary contrast structures: "Not X. It's Y." / "This is not X. It is Y."
- Dramatic sentence fragments used as reveals
- Adverbs (-ly words)
- Softeners: just, simply, actually, really, very, quite, perhaps, certainly
- Throat-clearing openers
- Meta-commentary: "Let me explain," "Here's why that matters"
- Passive voice
- Narrator-from-a-distance voice: "One might argue"
- Pull-quote-ready sentences
- Three-item lists (use two)
- Rhetorical questions immediately answered
- Negative listings: "Not because X. Because Y."

### Banned phrases (corporate buzzwords)
comprehensive, robust, holistic, seamless, crucial, game-changer, lean into, unpack, double down, navigate the landscape, deep dive, move the needle, at its core, in today's world, when it comes to, it turns out that, the reality is, what makes X unique, this is where it gets interesting

### Banned filler openers
"Here's the thing," "Let that sink in," "The uncomfortable truth is," "It's worth noting," "Let me be clear," "At the end of the day," "And that's okay"

### Always
- Vary sentence length
- State facts directly
- Trust the reader
- End paragraphs differently each time
- If it sounds like a pull-quote, rewrite it

### Self-check
- Three consecutive sentences same length → break one
- Paragraph ends with a punchy one-liner → vary it
- Em-dash before a reveal → remove it
- Explaining a metaphor → trust it to land
