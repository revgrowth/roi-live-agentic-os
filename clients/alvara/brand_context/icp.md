# ALVARA ICP

ALVARA's audience runs across multiple segments. Each segment has its own buying motion and content needs, with a different channel mix per segment. Campaign angles map to sub-segments inside the DTC subscriber segment; ambassador, wholesale, VitalityX, and foundation segments operate outside the angle map.

Source: Sections 3, 6, and 11 of `inputs/alvara-context-dump.md`.

---

## Segment 1: DTC subscriber (primary)

**Top-line:** 45-65, split gender, premium buyer, mature wellness audience.

**Pricing fit:** $108 one-time / $89 subscription. Holds against $30-40/mo category alternatives via the Foundation / Sanctuary / Symbiotic Stacking story.

**Buying motion:** Subscription-first. Single SKU at launch (MycoNectar). High consideration on entry; retention is the growth lever that backs out the 10K-subscriber-by-May-2027 plan.

**Sub-segments mapped to campaign angles:**

| # | Angle | Sub-segment |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Vitality Reclaimed | Men 45-65, energy / libido / testosterone focus |
| 02 | Radiance & Resilience | Women 45-65, hormonal balance / sleep / mood |
| 03 | The Reconnection Ritual | Couples 40-65, shared ritual / libido |
| 04 | Sleep That Restores | Adults 35-65, sleep-deprived |
| 05 | The Executive Edge | C-suite, sustained cognition (Lion's Mane focus) |
| 06 | Front Line Vitality | Veterans / first responders (Jamieson's story arc) |
| 07 | Biohacker | Secondary launch |
| 08 | Recovery / Athletes | Secondary launch |
| 09 | Corporate B2B | Microsoft pilot reference, $70K/month B2B target |
| 10 | Sustainability | Secondary launch |

**Compliance flag:** Angles 01 and 03 carry the highest risk for Meta account shutdowns. See `compliance.md` for ad-account risk mitigation by angle.

**Content needs:** Ingredient education hub pages, founder-led video in Ty's register, ritual content, lifecycle email and SMS via Klaviyo, comparison content vs MUDWTR / AG1 / Four Sigmatic, AEO-formatted answers for AI Overviews citation.

## Segment 2: Ambassador (five-tier partner channel)

ALVARA runs a five-tier partner channel: Broker, Affiliate, Wholesale, Distributor, Ambassador. Ambassador is the entry tier for community-led recommendation. Affiliate program management runs through UpPromote.

**Top-line:** Existing customer or community member who runs personal-network recommendation flow with revenue share. Highest-volume tier, lowest-commitment tier.

**Buying motion:** Recommendation flow. Ambassador pulls a referral link, posts in personal channels, earns commission per converted subscriber.

**Content needs:** Onboarding kit, story-led brand education, ritual content for personal use, recommendation-script library for organic social posting, monthly performance email.

**Voice mapping:** Ty's register for product education and ritual content; Jason / ROI.live register for performance data and operational communication.

---

## Segment 3: Wholesale buyer

Wholesale tier sits within the five-tier partner channel. Buyer profile: independent grocery, wellness boutique, naturopathic clinic, or boutique gym serving the 45-65 premium audience.

**Buying motion:** Direct-account purchasing with margin structure and minimums. Longer sales cycle than DTC. Often founder-led or Jamieson-led conversation.

**Content needs:** Wholesale line sheet, founder story for retail education, point-of-sale collateral, B2B pricing architecture (see `samples.md` 5.4 / Strategic Launch Readiness item 24).

**Voice mapping:** Jared's register for B2B framing, Jamieson's register where the buyer leans veteran-owned or veteran-serving.

---

## Segment 4: VitalityX prospect

VitalityX is the 16-week program inside the ALVARA brand structure. The prospect is an existing DTC subscriber, ambassador, or partner-channel member who is ready to step into a structured 16-week protocol alongside the daily ritual.

**Buying motion:** High-consideration purchase, longer cycle, often founder-led conversation. Higher price point than the standard subscription.

**Content needs:** Program overview, results frameworks, eligibility content, founder-led video assets walking through the 16-week arc, application or intake flow, post-program retention path back into standard subscription.

**Voice mapping:** Ty's register for the philosophy and protocol explanation; Jared's register for application and program-architecture detail.

---

## Segment 5: Foundation donor

ALVARA Foundation is a 501(c)(3) inside the brand structure. The donor profile is high-net-worth aligned with regenerative agriculture, indigenous-medicine preservation, or veteran wellness. Donors overlap with the DTC subscriber segment.

**Buying motion:** Charitable giving. Dedicated reporting cadence and impact-framing requirements. Tax-deductible.

**Content needs:** Foundation-specific narrative, Sanctuary footprint reporting, regenerative beekeeping documentation, veteran-program impact, annual donor letter.

**Voice mapping:** Jared's register for partnership framing and donor-direct communication; Jamieson's register where the angle crosses into Front Line Vitality and veteran-program work.

---

## Cross-segment notes

**Pricing fit varies.** DTC subscription holds at $89/mo. Ambassador and wholesale operate on margin / share. VitalityX and foundation run separate commercial structures.

**Content production overlap.** The video shoot creative brief (`samples.md` 5.5) is built to produce assets that serve all five segments from one full-day production. Founder portraits, farm and apiary coverage, and product ritual sequences flex across DTC, ambassador, foundation, and B2B applications.

**Voice routing:** See `voice-profile.md` for the full mapping. DTC and ambassador content runs in Ty's register by default. Wholesale and VitalityX content runs in Jared's register for B2B framing. Foundation content runs in Jared's register or Jamieson's register where the angle crosses into Front Line Vitality.
