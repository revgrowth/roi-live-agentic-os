## 4. The Growth Math

10,000 active subscribers × $89/month = **$890K MRR / $10.7M ARR** run rate.

12-13 month window from current date. Premium wellness category churn typically runs 5-8%/month until the brand earns loyalty. Gross adds need to ramp into the high hundreds, then well over 1,000/month near goal to compensate for steady-state churn.

### Critical framing
Platform ROAS captures only first-purchase revenue inside a 7-day attribution window. MER and LTV-adjusted ROAS are the accurate performance measures for supplement subscription brands. ## 7. Brand Voice and Writing Rules

These rules apply to every deliverable, including scripted founder dialogue.

### Never use
- Em dashes (any kind)
- Binary contrast structures: "Not X. It's Y." / "This is not X. It is Y."
- Dramatic sentence fragments used as reveals
- Adverbs (-ly words)
- Softeners: just, simply, actually, really, very, quite, perhaps, certainly
- Throat-clearing openers
- Meta-commentary: "Let me explain," "Here's why that matters"
- Passive voice
- Narrator-from-a-distance voice: "One might argue"
- Pull-quote-ready sentences
- Three-item lists (use two)
- Rhetorical questions immediately answered
- Negative listings: "Not because X. Because Y."

### Banned phrases (corporate buzzwords)
comprehensive, robust, holistic, seamless, crucial, game-changer, lean into, unpack, double down, navigate the landscape, deep dive, move the needle, at its core, in today's world, when it comes to, it turns out that, the reality is, what makes X unique, this is where it gets interesting

### Banned filler openers
"Here's the thing," "Let that sink in," "The uncomfortable truth is," "It's worth noting," "Let me be clear," "At the end of the day," "And that's okay"

### Always
- Vary sentence length
- State facts directly
- Trust the reader
- End paragraphs differently each time
- If it sounds like a pull-quote, rewrite it

### Self-check
- Three consecutive sentences same length → break one
- Paragraph ends with a punchy one-liner → vary it
- Em-dash before a reveal → remove it
- Explaining a metaphor → trust it to land

### Voice samples in Ty's register
"A mycelial network does not operate in isolation. It distributes nutrients across an entire forest floor through interconnected pathways. That is the design principle behind MycoNectar."

"Eight functional mushrooms. Sixteen supporting adaptogens and minerals. One raw honey base. Every compound in the formulation was selected because it strengthens the effect of another compound in the blend."

(Note: the second sample uses three short sentences that read close to dramatic fragments. Ty's voice tolerates this register. When writing in Jason's voice or ROI.live's voice, hold the rules tighter.)

## 8. Compliance and Regulatory

ALVARA is a dietary supplement with structure/function claims (stress resilience, cellular energy, immune adaptability, deep sleep). FDA disclaimer is on the product page.

### FDA / FTC red lines
- "Supports healthy testosterone levels" → compliant
- "Boosts testosterone" → gray area
- "Treats low testosterone" → violates FTC, triggers warning letters and ad account shutdowns

Every ad creative, landing page, email sequence, and social post touching health benefits goes through compliance review before publication. Build the regulatory checkpoint into the content approval workflow.

### Comparative claims to verify
"3× more potent than competitors" appears on the homepage as of last review and is currently unsourced. This needs sourcing or removal before any external content references it.

### Testimonial claims
Strong testimonials currently live on ALVARA's site as user testimonials ("testosterone went back up," "regulate my moon cycle"). Fine in that context. If picked up as evidence in content ROI.live writes, source and verify carefully. FTC has been active in these areas.

### Ad account risk
Reconnection Ritual (couples / libido) and Vitality Reclaimed (testosterone / energy for men) carry the highest risk for Meta account shutdowns. Google Ads supplement certification required. Mitigation: separate high-risk angles into dedicated ad accounts, warm slowly, maintain backup Business Manager access, write softer in paid channels and direct on owned channels.

### Trademarks
ALVARA, MycoNectar, The Nectary all need trademark filings before traction creates squat risk. $250-$350 per mark through USPTO. Investor due diligence will ask about IP protection.
