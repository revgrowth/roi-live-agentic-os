02 / The B2B Gap

What the MOU never covered.

MOU §5.9 was negotiated against the architecture we had on the table at the time: Advanced Shopify with a third-party B2B app — SparkLayer, BSS, or Wholesale Club bolted on top of standard Shopify. The $16K covered DTC theme work, subscription widget, and landing pages. It did not cover the parallel wholesale motion, because the wholesale motion was assumed to live inside an app.

The Shopify Plus memo we shipped April 27 made the case to abandon that path. Bolted-on B2B works at low volume and breaks at scale. The fix lives in the platform itself — Shopify's native B2B engine, which only exists on Plus. ALVARA is shipping DTC and wholesale in parallel from day one. That motion is what justifies Plus. It also means the build scope expands.

Why this surfaces now and not in the MOU

The Shopify platform decision was being treated as a January-2027 problem during MOU drafting — a "growth-stage" upgrade once volume justified it. The Plus memo flipped that: native B2B is a launch-architecture decision, not a scale decision. Doing it later costs more than doing it now. That changes what gets built before launch, which changes what the build line costs.

This is the right time to surface it. The MOU §5.9 language is open enough to absorb the shift — custom development is carved out of the retainer and priced separately. The number on that line just needs to reflect the actual scope. The structure (50/50 payment schedule, retainer unchanged, Maintenance tier at $1,200/mo on top) all carries forward without renegotiation.
