02 / The ADA Bonus

Why we're absorbing the ADA work.

ADA compliance for ecommerce is a moving target with real legal teeth. Web accessibility lawsuits crossed 4,600 federal filings in 2024 alone, with median settlement costs at $25,000 before legal fees. Most brands address it after the first demand letter arrives — meaning during the busiest growth windows, with retroactive remediation costing 3–4× the price of building it in from the start.

Building it in during the Shopify development phase is when this work is cheapest. The architecture decisions, the component patterns, the alt-text protocol — all of it gets baked in before the storefront goes live. Doing it after launch means rebuilding theme components and re-auditing every product page that ships in the meantime.

Why this matters strategically for ALVARA

The 45–65 demographic ALVARA targets has the highest accessibility-needs prevalence of any consumer cohort. ~40% have at least one accessibility consideration (vision, motor, cognitive). Building compliant from day one expands the addressable subscriber base materially in the exact demographic where the brand needs the runway. The legal protection is a side benefit — the market expansion is the real return.
