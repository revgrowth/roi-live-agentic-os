# Cluster Expansion Opportunities (Beyond the Proposal's 7)

**Run date:** 2026-04-27
**Source:** DataForSEO research + competitor audit + brand-context fit analysis
**Filter applied:** Real volume, FBC has authentic authority, low-medium organic difficulty, intent maps to a defensible content artifact (not just product page).

---

## Selection criteria

A keyword cluster is expansion-worthy if all four are true:

1. **Volume threshold** — head term ≥ 1,000/mo OR cluster total ≥ 3,000/mo summed.
2. **Difficulty fit** — KD ≤ 30 (most pillars KD ≤ 15).
3. **Authority fit** — FBC has *named authority* (a person, a process, a place, a product, a year-count) that competitors cannot replicate without doing the same work.
4. **Strategic fit** — content drives revenue (product, tour, subscription) OR drives authority that compounds for 2027+ rankings.

The transactional intent here is high. Even if a reader doesn't read the full guide, they'll click into product collections — so internal links should be aggressive: each product mentioned in the guide should link to its category page.

| **Chocolate Gifts (evergreen pillar)** | Q4 (October, before seasonal pages) | 12,000+/mo. Lifts the existing seasonal pages already in proposal. |

### Strategic re-prioritization (existing roadmap items)

- Move **"Fair Trade vs. Direct Trade"** from 2027 → Q3 2026 (volume justifies; supports the Sustainability pillar's keyword stretch into "fair trade chocolate" 9,900/mo).
- Move **"Hot Chocolate & Drinking Chocolate Recipes"** from Q3 supporting article → Q2 priority (or pull out to its own micro-pillar — Option A above).
- Re-target the **"Chocolate Pairing Guide" pillar** to anchor on "chocolate and wine" (8,100/mo) rather than "chocolate pairings" (110/mo).
- Re-target the **"Complete Guide to Chocolate" pillar** away from "what is dark chocolate" (1,300/mo, KD 25) toward "best chocolate brands" (1,600/mo, KD 2) OR rebuild as a hub around "what is a bonbon," "dark vs milk chocolate," "chocolate bonbons" interlinked architecture.
- Demote **"Direct Trade Cacao"** as a standalone supporting article (only 70/mo) → fold as a sub-section of the Ethical Chocolate Guide pillar.

### Volume math — before vs after

- **Proposal as-is** — 7 clusters, ~32 named pieces in 2026, head-term volume sum ≈ 22,000/mo across all pillar phrases (excluding Asheville geo-specific).
- **Proposal + recommended adjustments** — same 7 clusters re-pointed at high-volume terms + 5 expansion micro-clusters, head-term volume sum ≈ 110,000+/mo.

That is approximately a **5x increase in addressable monthly search volume** across the same content production cadence (8 pages/month). Most of the increase comes from a single move: routing the Recipes cluster to capture "hot chocolate recipe" (74,000/mo).
