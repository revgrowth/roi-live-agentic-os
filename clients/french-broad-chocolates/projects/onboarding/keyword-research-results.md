## Notes on AEO (AI search) intent

The DataForSEO API returns `main_intent` and `foreign_intent` flags that map closely to AI-citation intent. Key patterns:

- **Informational + commercial mixed intent** — these are the highest-value AI-citation targets, because AI assistants increasingly generate "answer + recommendation" responses. Examples: "ethical chocolate" (commercial + informational + transactional), "fair trade chocolate" (informational), "what is dark chocolate" (commercial).
- **Pure transactional** — these are best served by product pages, not content articles. Examples: "chocolate bonbons", "chocolate gifts", "chocolate subscription".
- **Pure informational with FAQ-shape** — these are the highest-value AEO targets for inclusion in AI Overview / answer-engine snapshots. Examples: "what is bean to bar chocolate", "what is a bonbon", "how chocolate is made", "dark chocolate vs milk chocolate".

For every pillar + supporting page, the AEO content recipe is:
1. Open with a 1-2 sentence direct answer (AI snippet bait).
2. Follow with structured H2s mapping to the question's natural sub-questions.
3. Embed FAQ schema explicitly.
4. Cite named experts (Jael / Dan) with org schema markup.
5. Include named producers / numbers / dates that AI models can attribute back to FBC.
