## Cross-service dependencies

- **Website Build → SEO / AEO / GBP:** Site architecture, schema, and on-page content are the substrate for organic performance. Phase 1 launch unblocks aggressive AEO posture.
- **Website Build → Ad Creative + Branding:** Landing-page templates and visual system are produced inside the website build but reused by Google Ads, META Ads, and Email.
- **Data + Attribution → FCMO:** FCMO numbers are only as trustworthy as the standardized attribution model. May 2026 cycle was the first to apply the standardization SOP.
- **Data + Attribution → Google Ads + META Ads:** Both paid channels feed the attribution model and depend on its taxonomy for reporting.
- **Brand voice / DNA Guide → SEO + Email + Organic Social + Ad Creative + Website Build + AI Brand Voice Agent:** Voice profile is the foundation document for every text-producing service. Sign-offs from Jérôme, Maureen, and Jeff unblock the AI Voice Agent in particular.
- **Branding → Ad Creative + Website Build:** Design tokens (color, type, logo) flow downstream into both. Raja owns continuity.
- **FCMO → all execution services:** Dashboard surfaces what's working and reallocates resources across the 10 other service lines.
