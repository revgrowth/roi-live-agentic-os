## Critical authoring rules (one-liners)

Per v1.1 §11.3-§11.4 and §18:

1. **Zero em dashes.** Replace with comma, period, parenthesis, or rewrite.
2. **No softeners, no throat-clearing, no meta-commentary.** Cut every -ly adverb except early, late, only.
3. **No three-item lists.** Use two items, or four-plus genuine items.
4. **No binary contrasts** ("It's not X. It's Y."). No dramatic one-word fragments.
5. **Form-first CTAs.** Phone in footer only. "Request a Free Consultation," never "Get Started Now."
6. **Sentence length varies.** No three consecutive sentences the same length.
7. **Name specifics, not abstractions.** Years, towns, suppliers, materials, equipment models.
8. **Voice attribution matches schema.** Author named in JSON-LD writes in their own voice.

## When to load the full guide

Load voice-profile-full-v1.1.md when:

- Drafting any page-level brief or content asset (pillar, cluster, location, blog, About).

- Answering a quick factual question (years, addresses, pillar URLs).
