### Q: How long does a typical project take from design to completion?

A: Project timelines depend on scope and complexity. A landscape planting project might take two to four weeks from design approval to completion. A full outdoor living space with a pool, hardscaping, and landscaping can take three to six months. Your designer will provide a detailed timeline during the proposal phase, and any changes to the schedule are communicated proactively.

_From: creative-briefs/faqs.md §2.4_

### Q: Can I break my project into phases?

A: Absolutely. Blue Tree regularly works with homeowners to phase projects over time. Your designer will identify the base platform — the structural and drainage elements that need to happen first — and then schedule subsequent phases like lighting, water features, plantings, or additional hardscape when you are ready. This approach keeps the overall design cohesive while making the investment more manageable. It is one of the most common ways our clients approach larger projects.

### Q: What warranties cover my new pool?

A: Blue Tree's pool warranty covers a lifetime structural warranty on the pool shell, plus two-year coverage on plumbing, electrical, gas, tile, coping, and veneer. Decking is limited one year. Plaster is one year for white plaster and ten years for our Quality Finish option. Pool equipment from Jandy comes with a three-year workmanship warranty. Plaster exclusions include normal color variation, scaling from pH imbalance, minor crazing, and texture unevenness, characteristics of hand-applied finish. Full warranty terms are published in our Service Hub.

_From: voice-profile-full-v1.1.md §17.3_

### Q: How long does it take to fix a bad lawn?

A: Lawn improvement is a process, not an overnight fix. Most lawns show visible improvement after one full season of professional care, but achieving a consistently thick, healthy lawn typically takes two to three years of ongoing treatment. The timeline depends on the starting condition, soil health, shade coverage, and how consistently you follow watering and mowing recommendations between treatments. Our turf care manager, Mark Peasley, works with homeowners to set realistic expectations for each year of the program.

_From: creative-briefs/faqs.md §2.8_

### Q: Is there a warranty on grass seed?

A: There is no formal warranty on grass seed, because germination depends on environmental factors outside anyone's control — watering consistency, temperature, rainfall timing, and soil conditions. That said, doing right by our customers matters more than fine print. Blue Tree follows up on all seeding work as standard practice, and if results are not where they should be, we work with you to address it. This approach reflects a long-term relationship philosophy: the better your lawn performs, the longer you stay with us.

### Q: Will my patio crack in the winter?

A: Pavers are individually laid units — unlike poured concrete, they are designed to flex with the freeze-thaw cycles that are standard in Southeastern PA. A properly installed paver patio with an engineered base and appropriate drainage should not crack. If individual pavers do become damaged or shift, they can be replaced individually without disturbing the surrounding installation. This is one of the key advantages of paver construction over poured concrete.

_From: creative-briefs/faqs.md §2.7_
_Note: Contains em dash._

### Q: What warranty covers my new plants?

A: Blue Tree provides a one-year, one-time replacement warranty on all plant material from the date of installation. If a plant dies within the first year due to weather conditions — such as an extreme heat wave or unusually harsh winter — we replace it at no cost. The warranty does not cover plant loss caused by homeowner neglect, including overwatering, underwatering, or failure to follow the care instructions we provide at project completion.

_From: creative-briefs/faqs.md §2.6_
_Note: Contains em dash._

### Q: Does Blue Tree install irrigation systems?

A: Blue Tree installs drip irrigation systems as part of landscape projects to support the health of new plantings. Drip irrigation delivers water directly to root zones, reducing waste and promoting stronger plant establishment. Your system will include controller instructions and winterization guidance. Blue Tree does not install sprinkler systems — our irrigation focus is on drip systems designed specifically for the landscape we install.

### Values section — six values (Our Story page)

**Reputation Over Revenue.** Jeff's father told him, "All you have in life is your reputation." That philosophy drives every decision — from which projects we take on to which materials we spec. We'd rather lose a job than cut a corner.

**Relationships, Not Transactions.** We're not trying to turn and burn. We want to be your outdoor living partner for life. Some of our client relationships stretch back 25 to 30 years. We're now doing work for the children of clients we started with.

**Transparency First.** We tell you what things cost, how long they'll take, and what could go wrong — before we start. When unknowns arise mid-project, we pick up the phone before we pick up a shovel. No surprises.

**Do It Right or Don't Do It.** When we added pool construction around 2011, we didn't wing it. We hired a builder with over 20 years of experience, partnered with Jandy for premium equipment, and accepted that the first few years wouldn't be profitable. That's the standard for everything we do.

**Adapt and Survive.** From the 2008 recession to COVID-19, we've proven we can pivot without compromising. Four-day workweeks to save jobs. Safety protocols to keep crews working. Agility isn't a buzzword here — it's a survival skill.

**Invest in People.** Our average employee tenure is 13 to 14 years. In an industry with brutal turnover, that number says more about our culture than any mission statement ever could. We grow careers here, not just lawns.
