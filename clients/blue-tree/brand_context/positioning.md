## Positioning statement

Blue Tree Outdoor Living designs and builds custom pools, landscapes, and hardscapes for homeowners across Southeastern Pennsylvania, with in-house design, construction, and maintenance under one roof since 1983. (v1.1 §1.4)

## Core promise

"We build what we design." Jeff Mattiola.

One firm holds the project from the first consultation through years of ongoing care. (v1.1 §1.3)

## Brand essence

Blue Tree is the full-service design and build firm that owns the complete outdoor living experience for affluent Southeastern Pennsylvania homeowners who refuse to coordinate three contractors. One company. One point of contact. One accountable party for design, permitting, excavation, construction, planting, lighting, drainage, restoration, and ongoing maintenance. (v1.1 §1.2)

## Competitive differentiators (5)

These differentiators are sourced from why-choose-blue-tree.md, v1.1 §6.3, and v1.1 §15. Each is provable, not aspirational.

### 1. Turnkey design and build under one roof

Most outdoor projects require three or four contractors: a pool builder, a landscaper, a hardscape installer, and somebody to fix the drainage when those three don't talk to each other. Blue Tree's five service divisions operate under one leadership team. The pool designer coordinates with the landscape crew. The hardscape installer knows where the lighting conduit needs to go. The drainage plan accounts for the patio, the pool, and the grade because the same team designed all of it. (v1.1 §6.1, why-choose-blue-tree §2.5)

### 2. Designers, not closers

The person who sells the job is the person who designs it and the person who walks the job site during construction. Eight or more degreed designers and sales professionals on staff hold degrees in landscape architecture, horticulture, and landscape contracting from Penn State, Rutgers, Temple University, Penn College of Technology, and Delaware Valley College. Over 100 years combined design experience. Free consultations and 3D renderings of the full property come before any commitment. Jérôme Besnard's framing: "We're more designers than salesmen." (v1.1 §6.1 Tier 2, §15.2)

### 3. 43 years standing behind the work

Founded 1983. Same ownership team since 1994 (Jeff Mattiola and Chad Ochnich). Operating from Skippack since 2008. 70 to 90 seasonal employees with a 13 to 14 year average tenure. Survived 2008 and COVID without outside capital. Aggie Kriebel has been on staff since 2008; Jose Zavala since 2011; Mark Peasley nearly two decades. The team that builds your pool is the team you call in ten years. (v1.1 §1.6, §1.7, §15.1)

### 4. Published warranty stack, not "comprehensive coverage"

The warranty is the proof. Blue Tree publishes the full matrix front and center: lifetime structural on pool shells; 10-year limited on Quality Finish plaster; 3-year workmanship on Jandy equipment; 2-year workmanship on hardscape, plumbing, electrical, tile, coping, and low-voltage lighting; 1-year, one-time replacement on plant material; re-treatment guarantee on mosquito and tick. Plaster exclusions are named, not hidden. The act of publishing is the differentiation. (v1.1 §7.3, why-choose-blue-tree §2.10)

### 5. In-house permitting, drainage, and restoration

Pool-only builders sell from coping in and leave grading, drainage, and lawn restoration to the homeowner. Blue Tree handles township permits, impervious surface calculations, engineering review, variance applications, final grading, seed, and straw. The cost that arrives after the shell is in is the cost Blue Tree quotes upfront. (v1.1 §10.1, §6.3, custom-pool-design-construction §1.4)

## Who Blue Tree is NOT for

Per v1.1 §9 and §10:

- Customers shopping for the lowest price only. Blue Tree is rarely the cheapest quote. The pitch is value over price.
- Customers wanting above-ground pools, vinyl liner pools, or fiberglass pools. Blue Tree builds concrete (gunite or shotcrete) only.
- Customers wanting full lawn sprinkler systems or smart irrigation controllers. Blue Tree installs drip irrigation for new landscape installs and refers sprinkler work to specialists.
- Customers wanting tree care beyond stump grinding and minor removal. Blue Tree refers canopy work and tree health to partner tree specialists.
- Customers wanting pool emergency repair as a standalone service. Referred to specialists.
- Commercial landscaping clients at launch. Residential focus; commercial may return later.
- Customers who treat contractors disrespectfully or demand mid-project changes that should have been resolved at design. Jeff calls these "nightmare clients" and Blue Tree disqualifies them on the first visit. (v1.1 §10.1)

## Messaging hierarchy

Per v1.1 §6.1:

- **Tier 1 — Headline (one-sentence master message):** Blue Tree Outdoor Living is the 43-year design and build firm that handles your pool, landscape, hardscape, and ongoing care under one roof, with degreed designers managing every project from first sketch through years of maintenance.

- **Tier 2 — Body (three pillars of differentiation):**
  1. Turnkey design and build. One company. One point of contact. Pool, landscape, hardscape, lighting, drainage, restoration, and ongoing care.
  2. Designers, not closers. Eight or more designers and sales professionals on staff with over 100 years combined experience. Free consultations and free 2D / 3D renderings of the whole backyard before you commit.
  3. 43 years standing behind the work. Founded 1983. Same ownership team since 1994. Warranty stack covers lifetime structural on pool shells, two-year hardscape workmanship, one-year one-time replacement on plant material.

- **Tier 3 — Proof (service-level):**
  - Pools: Concrete construction (gunite or shotcrete). Jandy equipment. Lifetime structural warranty. 15-year track record (pool division launched 2011).
  - Landscapes: Degreed designers. 3D rendering. 90% locally-sourced plants. One-year, one-time replacement warranty.
  - Hardscapes: ICPI-certified installers (Chad Ochnich, James Gonczkowski, Justin Ryen). Two-year workmanship warranty. Local supplier relationships including A&B Natural Stone.
  - Healthy Yards: Two-decade industry veteran (Mark Peasley) leading turf care. Organic and synthetic mosquito and tick options. Tiered programs.
  - Premier Outdoor Services: Year-round maintenance for properties Blue Tree built or maintains.

## Positioning by service pillar

Derived from v1.1 §10 and each pillar's creative brief.

- **Pools (/pools/):** The pool builder that doesn't stop at coping. Custom concrete pools (gunite or shotcrete) with Jandy equipment, lifetime structural warranty, and 3D renderings of the full backyard before excavation. The pool, the deck, the landscape, the drainage, the lighting, and the restoration all under one roof. Pool division launched approximately 2011; 15 years of construction track record. Custom quote range $75K to $200K+ for the pool alone, $150K to $400K+ for backyard transformations including landscape and hardscape. Design and construction leads include Justin Acal (Rutgers Landscape Architecture), Christopher DiVito (Temple BSLA), Mike Wadsworth and John Kostesich (pool construction managers, combined 44+ years industry). (v1.1 §10.1, custom-pool-design-construction)

- **Landscapes (/landscapes/):** Designer-led landscape design and installation for USDA Zone 6b and 7a properties. Eight or more degreed designers on staff. 3D renderings before installation. 90% of plants from local growers. One-year, one-time replacement warranty. Drainage and grading included. Pricing $10K to $80K+. Lead designers include Fred Barberra (senior, plant authority), Andrew Mattiola, Stephen Roehm, Justin Acal, Christopher DiVito. Services not offered on this pillar: sprinkler system installation, smart irrigation controllers, tree care beyond stump grinding. (v1.1 §10.2, landscape-design-installation)

- **Hardscapes (/hardscapes/):** ICPI-certified hardscape design and installation. Patios, outdoor kitchens, retaining walls, pergolas, pavilions, fire features, walkways, driveways, pool decks. Two-year workmanship warranty. Local supplier relationships including A&B Natural Stone. Pool-deck specialty: slip resistance, heat retention, materials rated for wet surfaces. Chad Ochnich, James Gonczkowski, and Justin Ryen hold ICPI certification. Pricing $15K to $100K+. (v1.1 §10.3)

- **Healthy Yards (/healthy-yards/):** Always plural, never singular. Comprehensive turf care, fertilization, aeration and overseeding, weed control, mosquito and tick control, soil health, lawn renovation, and sports field management. Mark Peasley (Turfcare Manager, nearly two decades, Penn State) leads. Tiered seasonal program pricing $400 to $2,000+ annually. Plain-language education on what's wrong and why. Site visits before quoting, which is rare for the category. Organic and synthetic mosquito and tick options with re-treatment guarantee. (v1.1 §10.4)

- **Premier Outdoor Services (/premier-outdoor-services/):** Year-round, full-property maintenance for clients who want one company managing everything outdoors. Renamed from "Outdoor Concierge"; never use the legacy name. Mulching, seasonal cleanups, pruning, bed maintenance, monthly garden visits, drainage maintenance, hardscape upkeep, pool maintenance and service. Most economical for clients whose property Blue Tree designed and built because crews already know the property. Single invoice, single point of contact for everything outside the house. Operation Tags cross-reference (a weed control page lives in both Healthy Yards and Premier Outdoor Services filtered views). (v1.1 §10.5, §10.6)
