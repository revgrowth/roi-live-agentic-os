## SECTION 4 — VOICE PROFILE

### 4.1 Tone Characteristics
Per v1.1 §1.4 and §4.1:

- Authoritative without being corporate. Speaks from 43 years of field experience, not a boardroom.
- Direct and transparent on money. Names price ranges and what drives variation. Refuses the "call for pricing" dodge.
- Warm and relationship-oriented. Every project is the beginning of a long-term relationship.
- Low-pressure and collaborative. Designers lead with listening, not closing.
- Legacy-proud without being nostalgic. References 1983 as proof of staying power, not sentimentality.
- Self-aware about scope. Says "we don't do that" when asked about services Blue Tree doesn't offer.

### 4.2 Target Reader (as a persona)
The Established Southeastern PA Homeowner. Age 40 to 65, household income $150K+, owns 0.5 to 5+ acre property in one of the five counties (Montgomery, Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Philadelphia). Treats the outdoor project as a long-term investment. Refuses to coordinate three contractors. Researches three to twelve months before reaching out. Compares two to three contractors. Often previously burned by a contractor. Reading-level target: Grade 9 to 11 per v1.1 §11.7.

Secondary persona: The New Homeowner, age 35 to 50, recently purchased in Southeastern PA, often new construction or resale with aging landscape, $10K to $80K landscape-led with phased plan potential.

Full persona detail in `clients/blue-tree/brand_context/icp.md`.

### 4.3 Pronoun Rule
- [x] **Default:** no bare "we" in body copy — use brand name or named expert (Core Standards Phase 3.4)

Override is not active. Blue Tree operates a multi-author E-E-A-T model where named experts carry the voice. Bare "we" is replaced with "Blue Tree" or the named author's voice per v1.1 §14.
