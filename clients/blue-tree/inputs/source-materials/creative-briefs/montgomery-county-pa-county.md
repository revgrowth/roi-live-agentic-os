## 1\. Page Purpose & Strategic Role

### Why This Page Exists

The Montgomery County page is the **most important location page on the entire website**. Blue Tree Landscaping is headquartered in Skippack—in the geographic center of Montgomery County. This is the company's home turf. More Blue Tree projects have been completed in Montgomery County than in any other county, and the majority of the Tier 1 "Golden Circle" towns fall within its borders.

This page serves as the **parent geographic authority hub** for all Montgomery County town pages. It must:

1. **Rank** for "\[landscaping / pool builder / hardscaping\] + Montgomery County PA" searches.
2. **Get cited by AI answer engines** when users ask "who is the best landscaper in Montgomery County?"
3. **Pass geographic authority** downward to all child town pages through internal linking.
4. **Connect geographic intent to service intent** by linking visitors from "where" to "what."
5. **Establish the headquarters advantage** — Blue Tree lives and works here.
6. **Capture leads** from homeowners who search by county.

Town pages go deep on hyperlocal relevance — specific neighborhoods, landmarks, and community details. ## 2\. Target Audience

### Primary Persona: The Montgomery County Homeowner

*   **Who:** Homeowners in Montgomery County who own single-family residential properties. Most are in established neighborhoods with homes built between the 1950s and 2010s. Many properties sit on half-acre to two-acre lots with mature trees, aging landscapes, and room for outdoor living improvements.
*   **Age Range:** 35–65.
*   **Household Income:** $150K–$500K+ (Montgomery County includes some of the highest-income zip codes in Pennsylvania, particularly in Blue Bell, Gwynedd Valley, Lower Gwynedd, and Lafayette Hill).
*   **Mindset:** They value quality, expect professionalism, and prefer to work with a company that knows the local area. Many have been disappointed by out-of-area contractors.
*   **Trigger Events:** Outdated landscape needing refresh, new pool project, patio or outdoor kitchen addition, recurring turf issues, desire for ongoing professional maintenance.
*   **What They Search:** "Landscaping company Montgomery County PA," "pool builders near me," "best landscaper in Blue Bell," "hardscaping Skippack PA," "lawn care Collegeville."

### Secondary Persona: The Relocation Buyer

*   **Who:** Homeowners who have recently purchased in Montgomery County and are looking to customize the outdoor space. May have relocated from another state or from Philadelphia.
*   **Mindset:** Researching, comparing companies, reading reviews, looking for local expertise.
