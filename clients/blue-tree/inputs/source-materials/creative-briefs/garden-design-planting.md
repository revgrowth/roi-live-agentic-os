### \[H2\] Frequently Asked Questions About Garden Design & Planting

**How much does professional garden design and planting cost in Southeastern PA?**
Garden design and planting costs vary based on the scope of the project, the size of the planting areas, and the species selected. We provide a free on-site consultation and a transparent, itemized estimate before any work begins. Many homeowners choose to phase larger projects over multiple seasons to manage the investment comfortably.

**When is the best time to plant in Pennsylvania?**
Spring (April through early June) and fall (September through mid-November) are the ideal planting windows in Southeastern PA. Fall planting is especially effective because cooler temperatures and autumn rainfall help roots establish before winter dormancy. Our designers will recommend the optimal timing based on the specific plant material in your plan.

**Do you offer a warranty on plants?**
Yes. All plant material installed by Blue Tree Landscaping is backed by a one-year, one-time replacement warranty from the date of installation. This warranty covers plants that fail under normal growing conditions. If a plant does not survive due to homeowner over-watering or under-watering, the warranty would not apply—but we provide a detailed watering guide with every project to set you up for success.

**Can you redesign an existing garden without starting from scratch?**
Absolutely. Many of our projects involve refreshing or renovating an existing landscape rather than a full removal. Our designers evaluate what is healthy and worth preserving—mature shade trees, well-established evergreen anchors—and build a new design around those assets while replacing outdated or overgrown material.

**Do I need to be home during the planting installation?**
You do not need to be present for the full duration of the installation. Your designer will walk you through the plan before work begins, and our crews are fully self-sufficient on-site. We will schedule a walkthrough upon completion to review the finished project and deliver your care instructions.

**What is the difference between garden design and landscape design?**
Garden design focuses specifically on the selection, arrangement, and installation of living plant material—flowers, shrubs, trees, and ground covers. Landscape design is the broader discipline that also encompasses hardscape elements such as patios and walkways, grading, drainage, lighting, and irrigation. At Blue Tree, our designers are trained across all disciplines, which means your garden plan is always integrated with the complete property vision.

**How long does a typical garden installation take?**
Most residential garden installations are completed within one to three weeks, depending on the project scope. Larger projects that involve grading, soil amendment, and irrigation integration may extend that timeline. Your designer will provide a detailed schedule during the proposal process.
