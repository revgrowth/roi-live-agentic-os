## 3\. Project Engagement Terms

### 3.1 Project Agreements

All service engagements are governed by a written project agreement signed by both the client and Blue Tree. The project agreement supersedes any general information on this Site regarding scope, pricing, timelines, or deliverables.

### 3.2 Change Orders

Changes to the project scope after the agreement is signed are documented through a written change order process. Change orders describe the modification, the cost impact, and the timeline impact. No changes are executed without written client approval.

### 3.3 Permitting

Where services require township permits (pool construction, electrical, gas lines, structures exceeding height thresholds, impervious surface compliance), Blue Tree handles the permitting process on the client's behalf. Permitting timelines vary by municipality and are outside Blue Tree's control. Permit fees are the responsibility of the client and are itemized in the project agreement.

### 3.4 Site Conditions

Project pricing accounts for expected site conditions assessed during the consultation. Unforeseen conditions discovered during construction (rock, underground utilities, undisclosed drainage issues, soil contamination) may require scope adjustments. Blue Tree communicates any unforeseen conditions and associated cost impacts before proceeding with additional work.

### 3.5 Timeline Expectations

Project timelines provided in proposals and agreements are good-faith estimates based on the scope, complexity, and conditions known at the time. Timelines may be affected by weather, permitting delays, material availability, and unforeseen site conditions. Blue Tree communicates timeline changes as they arise.
