# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This is the single most important trust-building page on the entire website. Unlike service pages that answer "what do you offer?", this page answers the deeper question every prospect asks before signing a five-figure contract: **"Can I trust these people with my home?"**

The Our Story page is also the canonical source of truth about Blue Tree as an organization entity. When Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or any AI system needs to answer "Who is Blue Tree?" or "Who is Blue Tree Landscaping?" — this is the page they should cite.

## 1.2 Conversion Goals

| Priority | Goal | Metric |
| ---| ---| --- |
| 1 | Convert brand-research visitors into consultation requests | CTA clicks → `/request-estimate/` |
| 2 | Establish Blue Tree Outdoor Living as a verified, trustworthy organization entity | AI citation rate, Knowledge Panel accuracy |
| 3 | Reduce buying objections through authentic storytelling | Scroll depth, time on page, bounce rate |
| 4 | Support trust equity for all other pages via internal linking | Inbound link referrals from this page |

## 1.3 Target Audience

This page is visited by people in the **trust-verification stage** of their buying journey:

*   Homeowners who Googled the company name after seeing a truck, sign, or ad
*   Prospects comparing Blue Tree against two or three other contractors
*   Visitors who received a proposal and are doing due diligence before signing
*   Spouses or family members who weren't at the initial consultation and want to vet the company
*   Real estate agents or builders looking for a reliable contractor to recommend

## 1.4 Conversion Psychology

These visitors are exhibiting high-trust-seeking behavior. They've moved past "what do you offer?" and are now asking "who are you?" The page must:

*   **Mirror their values** — family, quality, reliability, long-term thinking
*   **Reduce perceived risk** — 40+ years in business, 70–90 employees, warranties, generational clients
*   **Create identification** — "These are my kind of people"
*   **Provide social proof at peak receptivity** — employee tenure, Google rating, client stories
*   **Offer low-friction next steps at peak trust moments** — not just a CTA at the bottom

## 2.3 Introduction / Origin Section

**H2:** How Blue Tree Landscaping Began

**Body Copy:**

Blue Tree Outdoor Living is a full-service residential design-build firm headquartered in Schwenksville, Pennsylvania, serving homeowners throughout Southeastern PA — including Montgomery, Bucks, Chester, Delaware, and Philadelphia Counties. Originally founded as Blue Tree Landscaping in 1983 and now operating as Blue Tree to reflect the full scope of its outdoor living services, the company designs, builds, and maintains custom outdoor living spaces including [landscape design and installation](http:///landscapes/), [hardscape](http:///hardscapes/) [design and](http:///hardscapes/) [construction](http:///hardscapes/), [custom inground pools](http:///pools/) design and installation, [turf care programs](http:///healthy-yard/), and [ongoing property maintenance](http:///premier-outdoor-services/). Blue Tree employs 70 to 90 professionals with an average employee tenure of 13 to 14 years, and is led by co-owners Jeff Mattiola and Chad Ochnich, who have been partners for over 30 years.

But the numbers don't tell you why we do this. The story does.

Blue Tree started in 1983 as a small landscaping operation — an outgrowth of the Blue Tree Garden Center in Southeastern Pennsylvania. For several years, it was a way to pay for college and cover living expenses. Design-build work on a shoestring.

Jeff Mattiola joined the company in 1989. Already involved in landscaping before Blue Tree, Jeff had been managing a restaurant but knew outdoor living was where he belonged. Armed with a degree from the University of Pittsburgh, he saw the potential and made the leap. Within three years, he'd bought out the original partner and was running the business on his own.

There was no safety net. No outside investors. No business plan sitting in a filing cabinet. Just grit, determination, and what Jeff calls "the sheer desire of success."

**Pull Quote (prominent visual treatment):**
> "My dad always said to me, 'All you have in life is your reputation. Don't mess it up.' That stuck with me. It's guided everything we do." — Jeff Mattiola, Owner

Jeff's family was his lifeline in those early years. His parents gave him a place to stay so he could pour every dollar back into the business instead of rent. His father, after retiring, would come hang out at the shop — one of Jeff's favorite memories from that era.

Jeff wanted to build something that would last.

**Visual Direction:**

*   Two-column layout: story text on one side, historical photo(s) on the other

*   Mobile: Single column, full-width photo between text blocks

**CTA Placement:** After the dad's quote — this is a peak emotional resonance moment. Subtle inline CTA: "Ready to work with a team that puts reputation first? [Request a consultation →](http:///request-estimate/)"

## 2.7 Evolution Section — The Pool Story

For years, Blue Tree Outdoor Living worked alongside pool companies on residential projects. We'd handle the landscape before the pool went in, or clean up the mess after a pool-only contractor left. And we kept hearing the same frustration from homeowners:
