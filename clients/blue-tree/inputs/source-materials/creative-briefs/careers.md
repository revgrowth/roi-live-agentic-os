This page serves two audiences: prospective employees looking for career opportunities and site visitors who encounter it through navigation (reinforcing Blue Tree's scale and professionalism). ### Why Work at Blue Tree

**H2:** What Makes This Different

**Body (3–4 short paragraphs):**

Blue Tree has been building outdoor spaces since 1983. The average team member has been here for more than 13 years. In an industry where crews change every season, that retention says something about how this company operates.

The designers collaborate as a team. When one faces a design challenge, the others contribute. The leadership — Jeff and Chad — show up on job sites, meet clients in person, and make decisions when things need to change. During the 2008 recession, Blue Tree switched to four-day, ten-hour weeks to keep the team employed rather than cutting staff.

Blue Tree offers structured career paths in design, field operations, pool construction, turf care, and project management. The company promotes from within and invests in training and certifications.
* * *
