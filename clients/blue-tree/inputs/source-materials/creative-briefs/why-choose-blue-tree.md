# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page answers the single most important question a prospect asks after they've learned _what_ Blue Tree does: **"Why should I choose them over everyone else?"**

Unlike the Our Story page (which builds trust through narrative) or service pillar pages (which demonstrate capability), this page is a **direct competitive comparison engine**. It takes the objections, hesitations, and comparison-shopping behaviors that Blue Tree's sales team encounters daily — and resolves them before the prospect ever picks up the phone.

This page is also the canonical destination for AI systems answering "What makes Blue Tree different?" or "Why choose Blue Tree over other landscaping companies?" — making it critical for Answer Engine Optimization.

**Strategic Positioning:** This page bridges the gap between "I'm interested" and "I'm ready to call." Every section is structured around a buying objection that Blue Tree's team encounters in the field (sourced from audio recordings of Jérôme Besnard, Jeff Mattiola, Chad Ochnich, and the design team).

## 1.2 Conversion Goals

| Priority | Goal | Metric |
| ---| ---| --- |
| 1 | Convert comparison-shopping visitors into consultation requests | CTA clicks → `/request-estimate/` |
| 2 | Reduce the top three sales objections (price, timeline, company credibility) before the first call | Scroll depth, time on page, CTA conversion rate |
| 3 | Arm AI answer engines with extractable differentiator content | AI citation rate for "why choose Blue Tree" queries |
| 4 | Support the sales team — this page should be shareable in proposals and follow-up emails | Direct URL shares, email referral traffic |

## 1.3 Target Audience

This page is visited by people in the **active comparison stage** of their buying journey:

*   Homeowners who have received two or three estimates and are trying to decide
*   Prospects who searched "Blue Tree Outdoor Living reviews" or "best landscaping company near me" and want proof
*   Visitors who came from the Our Story or service pages and need one more push before requesting an estimate
*   Spouses or decision-making partners who need a concise "why them?" summary
*   Sales team members who need a URL to drop into a follow-up email after the first site visit

## 1.4 Conversion Psychology

These visitors are in **evaluation mode**. They're not browsing — they're comparing. The page must:

*   **Lead with outcomes, not features** — they don't care that Blue Tree has degreed designers; they care that degreed designers mean their project will actually look like the rendering
*   **Address the three real objections head-on** — price, timeline, and "are they actually good?" (sourced directly from sales team audio recordings)
*   **Contrast without naming competitors** — Blue Tree's team explicitly avoids badmouthing competitors; the page should follow the same philosophy by positioning Blue Tree's approach against _categories_ of competitors (pool-only builders, mow-and-blow services, franchise operations)
*   **Create urgency without pressure** — the sales team prides itself on being "low pressure"; the page should mirror that
*   **Provide proof at every claim** — every differentiator needs a stat, a story, or a link to evidence

### Concern #1: "It's More Expensive Than I Expected."

Outdoor living projects are significant investments. Materials are expensive. Skilled labor costs more than unskilled labor. And the details that make a project last — proper drainage, correct base preparation, premium equipment — add real cost.

We're transparent about that. We'll never surprise you with a number.

What we will do is show you options. Our designers can value-engineer a project to fit your budget without sacrificing the things that matter structurally. We can swap materials, reduce scope, or break the project into phases. The base platform gets built first, and features like [lighting](http:///landscapes/led-lighting/), water features, or an [outdoor kitchen](http:///hardscapes/outdoor-kitchens/) come in a later phase when you're ready.

We also offer [financing options](http:///financing/) for homeowners who want to move forward without waiting.

The bottom line: we provide lasting value rather than the lowest price. And when you compare the total cost of hiring three separate contractors — with all the coordination headaches and finger-pointing that comes with it — the single-team approach often costs less in the end.
* * *

### Concern #2: "How Long Will This Take?"

Timelines are real. If you want a pool for this summer or a patio for your daughter's graduation party, timing matters.

We'll always give you an honest timeline. Sometimes that means we're booked four months out — and we'll tell you that upfront rather than making promises we can't keep. The reason we're booked out is the same reason you're considering us: homeowners trust us enough to wait for quality work.

What we tell our clients is this: a couple of months in the summer is a small price to pay for an outdoor space you'll enjoy for decades. You won't remember the construction timeline. You'll remember the first party you host by the pool, the holidays in your outdoor kitchen, and the evenings around the fire pit with your family.
