Looking at the design mockup, I can see why the client loved it—the visual hierarchy and emotional storytelling flow is strong. Let me analyze what's working in the mockup versus the brief, address your keyword/headline concerns, and provide a revised creative brief.
## Analysis: What the Mockup Does Better
**The mockup's headline "Life happens outside – Let's make it beautiful"** is actually stronger than the brief's "Your Backyard Deserves to Be Legendary" because:
1. **It's relational, not transactional** — aligns with their brand voice ("we want to feel like we have a friendship when all is over")
2. **It positions them as partners** — "Let's" implies collaboration
3. **It's about lifestyle, not product** — connects to the emotional outcomes (memories, gatherings, life moments)
4. **It's humble yet confident** — not hyperbolic ("legendary" feels like every other contractor)
**The mockup's section flow is also better:**
*   "One Vision. One Team. Zero Headaches." — immediately addresses the pain point (juggling contractors)
*   "Driven by Design. Not Quotas." — directly from brand voice recordings (designers vs. salesmen)
*   Process section titled "Your Vision. Our Expertise. One Seamless Process."
* * *
## Keyword Strategy Recommendation
**Primary Homepage Target:** The brand entity itself — **Blue Tree Landscaping**
**Why brand-first on the homepage:**
1. Homepage should establish entity authority (Google's Entity SEO)
2. Branded searches convert at 2-3x non-branded
3. Answer Engine Optimization requires establishing "who is Blue Tree Landscaping" definitively
4. Service + location pages handle non-branded queries
**Title Tag Recommendation:**`Blue Tree Landscaping | Pools, Hardscaping & Outdoor Living | Southeastern PA`
**H1 Headline Strategy:** The H1 does NOT need to contain "Blue Tree Landscaping" — the logo, title tag, schema, and body copy handle brand signals. The H1 should be emotionally compelling and benefit-driven.
However, the **subheadline or trust badge area** should reinforce the brand + geo + tenure for AEO:
> "Blue Tree Landscaping has been transforming outdoor spaces across Southeastern Pennsylvania for over 40 years."
