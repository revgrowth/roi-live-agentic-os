## PAGE PURPOSE

This page removes the budget objection from the conversion funnel. Pool projects ($75K–$200K+), full backyard transformations ($50K–$150K+), and large hardscape projects ($30K–$100K+) represent major investments. Financing makes those projects accessible to homeowners who want the work but cannot or prefer not to pay in full upfront.

The page also functions as an E-E-A-T Trust signal — transparent discussion of payment terms and financing options demonstrates financial openness that builds buyer confidence.

This page is referenced by: the Request Estimate form ("Interested in financing?" checkbox), FAQ answers across all pillar pages (budget/phasing questions), and the pool cost blog post.

### Why Financing Exists Here

**H2:** Your Budget Should Shape the Timeline, Not the Vision

**Body:**

The best outdoor living projects are designed as a complete vision from the beginning — then built in phases or funded in a way that makes the investment manageable. Blue Tree's designers plan the entire project upfront so every element connects, whether you build it all at once or in stages over several years.

Financing allows homeowners to move forward with the full scope of their project sooner rather than phasing it over multiple years and multiple construction mobilizations. In many cases, financing the complete project costs less than splitting it into phases with separate site preparation, separate permits, and separate crew mobilizations.

### FAQ (4 Q&As)

**Q1: Can I finance a pool project?**
Yes. Pool projects are the most common financing use case at Blue Tree. Financing allows homeowners to build the complete backyard transformation rather than phasing it over multiple construction seasons.

**Q2: Does financing affect my project timeline?**
Financing approval is handled during the design phase, before construction begins. Once approved, financing does not delay construction.

**Q3: Can I combine financing with phased construction?**
Yes. Some homeowners finance the first phase (e.g., pool + essential hardscape) and self-fund later additions. Your designer plans the master vision upfront so both approaches work.

**Q4: How do I apply?**
Discuss financing during your free consultation. Your designer can walk you through the available options and connect you with the financing partner's application process. [Request a Consultation →](http:///request-estimate/)
