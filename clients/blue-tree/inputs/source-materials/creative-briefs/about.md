## CRITICAL DISTINCTION: ABOUT OVERVIEW vs. OUR STORY

This creative brief covers the **About Us overview page** at `/about/` — the **parent hub** for the entire About section. This page is fundamentally different from the Our Story page at `/about/our-story/`:

|  | About Overview (`/about/`) | Our Story (`/about/our-story/`) |
| ---| ---| --- |
| Purpose | Hub/navigation page that establishes Blue Tree as an Organization entity and routes visitors to the right child page | Narrative deep-dive into Blue Tree's founding, values, and 40-year journey |
| Content Style | Concise entity definition + structured navigation cards + trust proof | Long-form storytelling, pull quotes, personal anecdotes, emotional arc |
| Visitor Intent | "Tell me about this company — fast" | "I want the full story" |
| Design Feel | Clean, scannable, hub-style — closer to a landing page | Editorial, magazine-style — closer to a feature article |
| Word Count | ~1,200–1,500 words | ~3,500 words |
| Entity Role | Canonical Organization entity page — the page Google and AI engines should index as the definitive "about" resource | Brand narrative anchor — supports the Organization entity with depth, storytelling, and E-E-A-T signals |

Think of it this way: if a prospect Googles "Blue Tree Landscaping" and clicks the About link in the navigation, this is the page they land on. It should answer their first three questions — Who are you? What do you do? Why should I trust you? — within 10 seconds of scrolling, and then offer clear paths to dig deeper (Our Story, Meet the Team, Why Choose Us, Our Process).

This page does **not** try to tell Blue Tree's full story. That's Our Story's job. This page introduces the company, establishes credibility, and routes visitors to the deeper content they need.

# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY

## 1.1 Page Purpose

The About overview page serves four strategic functions simultaneously:

**1\. Organization Entity Canonical Page.** This is the page that establishes Blue Tree as a recognized Organization entity for search engines and AI answer engines. When Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or any AI system needs to resolve "Who is Blue Tree?" or "What is Blue Tree Outdoor Living?" — this page provides the structured, concise, schema-marked answer. While the Our Story page provides narrative depth, this page provides the definitive entity definition in a format optimized for extraction and citation.

**2\. About Section Navigation Hub.** This page is the parent node for five child pages: Our Story, Meet the Team, Why Choose Us, Our Process, and Editorial Standards. Its primary UX function is routing visitors to the right child page based on their specific trust-seeking question. A prospect comparing contractors needs Why Choose Us. A prospect who wants the personal story needs Our Story. A prospect vetting credentials needs Meet the Team. This page ensures nobody has to guess where to go.

**3\. Trust Signal Aggregator.** The About overview concentrates Blue Tree's most powerful trust signals — founding year, employee count, employee tenure, Google rating (live widget), certifications, service scope — into a single scannable section. Visitors who will never read 3,500 words on the Our Story page still absorb these proof points from the overview.

**4\. Internal Linking Architecture Node.** As the parent of five child pages and the sibling of every service pillar page, this page distributes link equity across the entire trust infrastructure. Every service page, location page, and blog post that links to `/about/` is also strengthening the authority of everything underneath it.

## 1.3 Target Audience

The About overview page sits at the intersection of multiple visitor types and intent levels:

### High-Intent Brand Researchers

*   Homeowners who Googled "Blue Tree Outdoor living" or "Blue Tree reviews" and clicked the About link
*   Prospects who received a proposal and are vetting the company before signing a five- or six-figure contract
*   Spouses or co-decision-makers who weren't at the consultation and need a quick company overview
*   Real estate agents or builders evaluating whether to recommend Blue Tree to clients

**What they need:** A fast, credible company summary — who you are, how long you've been doing this, how many people work here, what you do — followed by clear paths to deeper detail if they want it.

### Navigation-Driven Visitors

*   Site visitors who clicked "About" in the main navigation as part of a general site exploration
*   Visitors who landed on a service page, liked what they saw, and are now evaluating the company behind the services
*   Return visitors who've already explored services and are now in the trust-verification phase

**What they need:** A hub page that answers "What's in this section?" immediately and lets them self-select the right child page without guessing.

### AI and Search Engine Crawlers

*   Googlebot indexing the site's organizational structure
*   AI systems building entity profiles for answer generation
*   Google Quality Raters evaluating E-E-A-T signals across the site

**What they need:** Clear Organization schema, structured content, entity-rich copy in the first 200 words, and well-defined parent-child page relationships.

## 1.4 Conversion Psychology

The About overview page operates on a simple but often-misunderstood psychological principle: **most visitors don't want the full story — they want the highlights, fast.**

Research on how users interact with "About" pages shows that the majority of visitors scan rather than read. They're looking for validation signals — "Have they been around long enough? Are they big enough? Do they look professional?" — before deciding whether to invest attention in deeper content or move straight to contact.

This page must accomplish three things in the first screen (above the fold):

1. **Establish identity.** "Blue Tree is a \[what\] based in \[where\] that does \[what\] since \[when\]."
2. **Prove scale.** 70–90 employees, 40+ years, five service divisions — these numbers instantly communicate "this is a real company, not a guy with a truck."
3. **Offer clear next steps.** Both conversion CTAs (Request a Consultation) and navigation CTAs (Explore Our Story, Meet the Team) should be visible without scrolling.

The rest of the page exists for visitors who scroll — giving them a compact trust overview and structured paths to the specific trust content they're seeking.

# PART 2 — PAGE DESIGN & COPY

> **Design Philosophy:** This is a hub page, not a storytelling page. The design should feel clean, confident, and organized — like the lobby of a well-run company. Generous whitespace, clear visual hierarchy, and scannable content blocks. Every section should answer a question and then point the visitor to the next step. The emotional depth lives in the child pages (Our Story, Meet the Team); this page earns the click to get there.
