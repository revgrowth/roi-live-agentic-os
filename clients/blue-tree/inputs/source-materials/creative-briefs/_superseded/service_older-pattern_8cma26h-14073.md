# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY

## 1.1 Page Purpose

The Service Hub is the most underestimated page type on a contractor website — and one of the most strategically valuable. While competitors bury their warranty details in PDF footnotes and leave care instructions to a verbal "good luck" at project handoff, Blue Tree is building a permanent, searchable, filterable knowledge base that serves four critical functions simultaneously:

**1\. Post-Sale Trust Anchor.** The moment a contract is signed, buyer anxiety shifts from "Can I trust them to do the work?" to "What happens if something goes wrong?" The Service Hub answers that question before it's ever asked — signaling that Blue Tree stands behind its work with transparent warranties, accessible FAQs, and detailed care instructions.

**2\. Pre-Sale Differentiator.** Prospects comparing contractors will discover that Blue Tree is the only company in the market with a dedicated, organized resource center. Warranty transparency before the sale builds confidence that competitors who hide their terms cannot match. This is a conversion tool disguised as a support page.

**3\. Information Architecture Node.** The Service Hub connects Blue Tree's five service pillars to their corresponding post-project support resources through the Operation Tag system. It creates a navigational pathway that says: "We don't just build it — we support it, maintain it, and teach you how to care for it."

**4\. AI Citation Magnet.** When ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overview is asked "Does Blue Tree offer warranties?" or "How do I care for my new paver patio?" or "What does Blue Tree's pool warranty cover?" — the Service Hub and its children are the pages that should be cited. Structured, well-organized answer content on warranty terms and care procedures is exactly what AI engines extract and display.

## 1.2 Conversion Goals

| Priority | Goal | Metric |
| ---| ---| --- |
| 1 | Route visitors to the correct child resource (warranties, FAQs, or care instructions) | Click-through rate on child page cards/links |
| 2 | Convert pre-sale visitors researching warranties/guarantees into consultation requests | CTA clicks → `/request-estimate/` |
| 3 | Convert post-sale visitors into Premier Outdoor Services clients | CTA clicks → `/premier-outdoor-services/` |
| 4 | Reduce inbound support calls by providing self-service answers | Phone call reduction for routine warranty/care questions; time on page |
| 5 | Establish Blue Tree as the most transparent contractor in the market | AI citation rate for warranty and care-related queries |
| 6 | Support E-E-A-T trust signals across the entire site | Inbound link referrals from this hub to child pages and service pages |

## 1.3 Target Audience

The Service Hub serves two fundamentally different audiences at two different buying stages:

### Pre-Sale Visitors (Research & Comparison Stage)

*   Homeowners comparing Blue Tree against 2–3 other contractors, evaluating who stands behind their work
*   Prospects who searched "Blue Tree warranty" or "Blue Tree guarantee" after receiving a proposal
*   Visitors who clicked "Warranties" in the navigation out of curiosity — a trust-seeking behavior
*   Spouses or co-decision-makers doing due diligence before approving a five- or six-figure project

**What they need:** Confidence that Blue Tree's warranty coverage is real, specific, and accessible — not hidden in fine print. They want to see that Blue Tree has organized, professional support infrastructure.

### Post-Sale Visitors (Existing Clients)

*   Recent clients who just had a project completed and want to understand their warranty coverage
*   Long-term clients looking up care instructions for seasonal maintenance tasks
*   Homeowners with a question or concern about their project (leak, discoloration, plant health, paver settling)
*   Clients considering additional work and returning to the site for the first time since their last project

**What they need:** Fast, clear answers to specific questions — organized by the service they purchased, not buried in a generic FAQ. They want to find what they need in under 30 seconds.

## 1.4 Conversion Psychology

The Service Hub operates on a psychological principle that most contractor websites ignore: **post-sale transparency drives pre-sale confidence.**

When a prospect sees that Blue Tree publishes its warranty terms, organizes care instructions by service type, and provides a searchable FAQ library — all before a contract is signed — it signals three things:

1. **"They have nothing to hide."** Companies that bury warranty terms are telling you something. Companies that publish them front-and-center are telling you something too.
2. **"They've done this enough to systematize it."** Organized care instructions and categorized FAQs suggest a mature operation with repeatable processes — not a company figuring it out as they go.
3. **"They're still here after the check clears."** The existence of post-project resources signals long-term commitment. This directly addresses the #1 fear in home improvement: the contractor who disappears after the final payment.

For existing clients, the psychology is simpler: **reduce friction, build loyalty, and create upsell opportunities.** Every time a client self-serves on the Service Hub instead of calling the office, Blue Tree saves administrative time. Every care instruction that ends with "or let our Premier Outdoor Services team handle it for you" creates a natural upsell moment.
