### The Three Phases (Visual Timeline)

**Design Direction:** Three-phase horizontal timeline (desktop) or vertical stack (mobile). Each phase is a clickable section that expands or scrolls to detail.

**Phase 1 — Design**

Your project begins with a free on-site consultation. A degreed designer visits your property, listens to your goals, evaluates conditions (soil, grade, drainage, sun, existing structures), and creates a custom plan using CAD software and 3D renderings. You see the finished result on screen before we break ground. Changes happen in the design, not in the field.

Blue Tree's design team holds degrees from Penn State, Rutgers, Temple, and Penn College of Technology. They collaborate — when one designer faces a challenge, the others contribute. You get the collective experience of 100+ combined years on every project.

**Phase 2 — Build**

Construction follows the approved design. Your designer manages the project from start to finish — the same person who sat at your kitchen table is the person overseeing every crew on your site. Pools, landscape, hardscape, lighting, drainage, grading, and fencing are coordinated as one project under one point of contact.

Blue Tree handles permitting, material procurement, scheduling, and all subcontractor coordination (though most work is performed by in-house crews). Site restoration — grading, seeding, and clean-up — is part of every project. You are not left with a construction zone.

**Phase 3 — Maintain**

The project does not end at the walkthrough. Blue Tree's Premier Outdoor Services division provides ongoing care: pool opening and closing, bed maintenance, paver restoration, seasonal clean-ups, pruning, and year-round maintenance packages. The team that built your outdoor space is the team that maintains it — they know every plant, every paver, every piece of equipment because they installed it.
* * *

### How the Five Divisions Connect

**H2:** Five Divisions. One Team.

**Body:** Blue Tree's five service divisions — Pools, Landscapes, Hardscapes, Healthy Yards, and Premier Outdoor Services — operate under one roof. Your pool designer coordinates with your landscape architect. Your hardscape crew reports to the same project manager as your lighting installer. Your maintenance team has access to the original design plans.

This integration is managed through Blue Tree's Operation Tags system. Every project, every review, every team member, and every resource is tagged to the service divisions it involves. When you browse the portfolio filtered by "Pools," you see pool projects. When you visit the reviews page filtered by "Landscapes," you see landscape-specific testimonials. The tags connect everything.

### The Blue Tree Complete Backyard Process — Summary

**H2:** The Complete Backyard Process

**Body:** Blue Tree's named methodology applies across all service divisions. Whether the project is a pool, a patio, a landscape design, or a full backyard transformation, the process follows the same eight-step structure:

1. Discovery Consultation (free, on-site)
2. Custom Design & 3D Renderings
3. Material Selection & Guidance
4. Permitting (where required)
5. Site Preparation & Base Work
6. Construction / Installation
7. Landscape Integration & Site Restoration
8. Final Walkthrough & Warranty Coverage

Each pillar page documents how the process adapts to that service type. The structure is consistent; the details vary by project scope.

### FAQ (4 Q&As — lightweight)

**Q1: How long does the design process take?**
Design timelines range from 2–3 weeks for a patio or landscape project to 4–6 weeks for a full backyard transformation including a pool. Your designer presents the plan with 3D renderings so you can make informed decisions before construction begins.

**Q2: Who manages my project?**
Your designer. The person who created the plan manages the project from start to finish. No handoffs to a project manager you have never met.

**Q3: Do I need to coordinate with multiple contractors?**
No. Blue Tree handles pools, landscapes, hardscapes, lighting, drainage, grading, fencing, and ongoing maintenance in-house. One company. One point of contact.

**Q4: What happens after the project is finished?**
Your designer conducts a final walkthrough covering care instructions, warranty terms, and maintenance recommendations. Blue Tree's Premier Outdoor Services division is available for ongoing care. [Learn about Premier Outdoor Services →](http:///premier-outdoor-services/)
