### \[FAQ SECTION\]
**Section Headline:**
> Landscape Design Questions Answered
**FAQs (10):**
* * *
**Q: How much does landscape design cost in Pennsylvania?**
A: Costs vary significantly based on property size, scope, and plant selections. A foundation refresh might start around $5,000-$15,000, while comprehensive property landscaping can range from $25,000 to $100,000+. We provide detailed proposals with transparent pricing after our site consultation—no surprises.
* * *
**Q: What's the difference between a landscaper and a landscape designer?**
A: A landscaper typically focuses on installation and maintenance. A landscape designer creates the plan—analyzing your site, developing a cohesive design, specifying appropriate plants, and producing drawings. At Blue Tree, our designers are degreed professionals who both design AND oversee installation, so nothing gets lost in translation.
* * *
**Q: When is the best time to start planning a landscape project?**
A: Plan one to two months before you want installation completed. This allows time to develop a custom design, finalize the contract, and get on the schedule. If you're flexible on timing, fall is actually ideal for many projects—cooler temperatures and seasonal rains help plants establish before winter.
* * *
**Q: What's the best time of year to plant in Southeastern PA?**
A: Spring (April-May) and fall (September-October) are ideal planting seasons in our region. Fall is often preferred—cooler temperatures and fall rains help roots establish before winter dormancy. We can design anytime and schedule installation for optimal planting windows.
* * *
**Q: How long does a landscape project take?**
A: Timeline depends on project scope. A foundation planting might take 2-3 days to install. A full property landscape could take 2-4 weeks. Design development typically takes 2-3 weeks before installation begins. We'll provide a clear timeline in your proposal.
* * *
**Q: What's the difference between annuals and perennials?**
A: Annuals grow for one season and die after cold weather arrives—think marigolds, petunias, and impatiens. Perennials return year after year—like coneflowers, hostas, and salvia. Most landscape designs use a foundation of perennials for structure, with annuals for seasonal color pops.
* * *
**Q: Do you offer a warranty on plants?**
A: Yes. We provide a one-year, one-time replacement warranty on all plant material from the date of installation. If a plant fails due to conditions beyond your control (extreme weather, disease), we'll replace it. The warranty doesn't cover plants that die from improper watering—but we'll give you clear care instructions to prevent that.
* * *
**Q: How often should I water new plants?**
A: New plants need deep watering less frequently—not shallow daily watering. Generally, once or twice per week is sufficient, depending on weather and soil conditions. We provide detailed watering instructions for every plant we install, and we offer drip irrigation systems that automate the process.
* * *
**Q: Is an irrigation system necessary?**
A: Not strictly necessary, but highly beneficial. Drip irrigation ensures consistent, appropriate watering—especially critical in the first two years while plants establish. It also saves time and prevents the over/under watering that kills most new plantings. We can install drip irrigation as part of your landscape project.
* * *
**Q: What if I only have a partial budget right now?**
A: We design for phased implementation all the time. We can create a master plan for your entire property, then prioritize what gets installed first based on your budget and needs. Many clients start with the front yard or a specific problem area, then add phases over subsequent years.
