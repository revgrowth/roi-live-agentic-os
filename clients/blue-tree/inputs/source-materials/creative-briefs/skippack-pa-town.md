## 1\. Page Purpose & Strategic Role

### Why This Page Exists

The Skippack page is the **single most important town page on the entire website.** Blue Tree Landscaping's headquarters sits at 4494 Skippack Pike. This is the company's home. More Blue Tree projects have been completed within Skippack Township than in any other single town, and the company's deepest community relationships — generational client families, local sponsorships, and daily visibility — are rooted here.

This page serves as the **hyperlocal geographic authority page** for Skippack Township. It must:

1. **Rank** for "\[landscaping / pool builder / hardscaping\] + Skippack PA" and "near me" searches from users in Skippack.
2. **Get cited by AI answer engines** when users ask "who is the best landscaper in Skippack?" or "landscaping near Skippack Village."
3. **Inherit authority** from the parent Montgomery County page through internal linking.
4. **Pass topical authority** to any service+location hybrid pages (e.g., `/pools/skippack/`, `/hardscapes/skippack/`).
5. **Establish the ultimate headquarters advantage** — this is not a company that "also serves" Skippack. This is Skippack's landscaping company.
6. **Capture leads** from homeowners who search by town name or ZIP code (19474).

### Differentiation from the County Page

The Montgomery County page establishes Blue Tree as the dominant authority across the entire county — it introduces all towns, provides a service overview, and functions as the geographic pillar. **This town page goes deep.** It names specific Skippack neighborhoods, references local landmarks (Skippack Village, Perkiomen Creek, Evansburg State Park), addresses township-specific permitting, describes the soil and terrain conditions unique to this part of the county, and speaks with the familiarity of a neighbor — because that is exactly what Blue Tree is in Skippack.

### Differentiation from Other Town Pages

Skippack is unique among all town pages because it is the **headquarters town.** No other town page can claim "we are headquartered here." The copy on this page leans heavily into that advantage: the physical address, the daily presence, the proximity, the founding story that began on this soil. When this template is adapted for other towns, the headquarters language is replaced with proximity language ("Based in nearby Skippack, our team is minutes from your \[Town\] property.").
