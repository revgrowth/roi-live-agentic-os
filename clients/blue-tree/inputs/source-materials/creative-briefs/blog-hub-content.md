# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY

## 1.1 Page Purpose

The Blog Hub serves four strategic functions in the Blue Tree site architecture:

**1\. Topical Authority Aggregator.** Every blog post strengthens Blue Tree's topical authority within its service pillars. The Blog Hub page is the organizational layer that connects all educational content to the broader site architecture — linking posts UP to service pillar pages and LATERALLY to related cluster pages. For Google, this creates a clear content cluster signal: Blue Tree doesn't just sell pools and landscapes; it educates homeowners about them.

**2\. Top-of-Funnel Traffic Capture.** Blog content targets informational and consideration-stage queries — the "how much does..." "what is the difference between..." "how to choose..." questions that homeowners ask months before they're ready to request a consultation. These visitors may not convert today, but they enter the Blue Tree ecosystem. The Blog Hub ensures they can discover related content that deepens their engagement and moves them toward conversion.

**3\. AI Answer Engine Content Index.** When an AI system is asked "How much does an inground pool cost in PA?" or "Best plants for Southeastern PA landscapes," it needs to discover the specific blog post that answers that question. The Blog Hub — with its structured post listings, author attribution, topic categorization, and freshness signals — serves as the canonical discovery layer for AI crawlers. A well-structured Blog Hub increases the likelihood that Blue Tree's individual posts are indexed, cited, and linked by AI answer engines.

**4\. E-E-A-T Demonstration Layer.** The Blog Hub is where Blue Tree's editorial credibility is most visible. Every post listed on this page carries an author name linked to a credential-rich bio page, an Operation Tag connecting it to a specific service division, and a publication date demonstrating active, ongoing expertise. For Google Quality Raters evaluating E-E-A-T, this page is evidence that Blue Tree invests in original, expert-authored educational content — not generic marketing copy.

## 1.4 Conversion Psychology

The Blog Hub is a trust-building page, not a direct-conversion page. Its primary psychological function is to establish Blue Tree as the most knowledgeable, transparent, and helpful voice in the Southeastern PA outdoor living space. Visitors arriving here are in information-gathering mode — they want to learn, not buy.

The conversion strategy is indirect but powerful: every blog post educates the reader on a topic that ultimately reinforces why they should hire a professional (and specifically Blue Tree) for their project. A homeowner who reads "How Much Does an Inground Pool Cost in PA?" walks away understanding the complexity of pool construction — and understanding that Blue Tree is the kind of company that respects them enough to explain costs honestly rather than hiding behind "call for a quote."

The Blog Hub should feel like a curated library, not a sales floor. Clean organization. Clear topic categories. Visible authorship. No pop-ups, no aggressive CTAs above the fold, no "limited time offer" pressure. The trust is in the substance.

# PART 2 — PAGE DESIGN & COPY

> **Design Philosophy:** The Blog Hub should feel like a curated, well-organized editorial index — not a reverse-chronological blog roll. Think of it as a magazine's table of contents: organized by topic, highlighting featured content, and making it effortless for a visitor to find the article that matches their current question. The design should be clean, text-forward, and image-supported — never image-dominated. The content and the authors are the stars, not the visual design.

## 2.3 Operation Tag Filter Bar

**Design Direction:** Horizontal tab/filter bar matching the styling used on Portfolio, Reviews, and Service Hub pages. Provides topic-based navigation across all blog content.

**Tabs:**

| Tab | Filter Behavior |
| ---| --- |
| All Topics (default) | Displays all blog posts, most recent first |
| Pools | Filters to posts tagged with the Pools Operation Tag |
| Landscapes | Filters to posts tagged with the Landscapes Operation Tag |
| Hardscapes | Filters to posts tagged with the Hardscapes Operation Tag |
| Healthy Yards | Filters to posts tagged with the Healthy Yard Operation Tag |
| Premier Outdoor Services | Filters to posts tagged with the Premier Outdoor Services Operation Tag |

**Implementation Notes:**

*   Operation Tag filtering is CMS-driven — same taxonomy used across Portfolio, Reviews, Team, and Blog
*   Posts with multiple tags (e.g., "Hardscape vs. Softscape" tagged Hardscapes + Landscapes) appear in both filtered views
*   Active tab state should be visually distinct (filled background, bold text, underline, or similar) — match the styling established on the Reviews and Portfolio pages
*   Filter bar should be sticky on scroll (desktop) or remain accessible via a floating filter button (mobile)
*   Keyboard-accessible and screen-reader-friendly
*   When a filter is active, the URL should not change (no `/blog/?tag=pools` — avoid thin content / duplicate index issues). Use JavaScript-based client-side filtering or AJAX loading.

## 2.4 Featured Post Section

**H2:** Featured

**Design Direction:** A single featured post displayed prominently at the top of the content area, below the filter bar. Larger card treatment than standard posts — think "lead story" in a magazine layout. This section is editorially curated (not auto-populated by recency).

**Featured Post Card Layout:**

| Element | Description |

| Read time | Estimated reading time (e.g., "8 min read") |

**Launch Featured Post:** "How Much Does an Inground Pool Cost in PA?" — this is the highest-search-volume, highest-conversion-potential post in the launch set. It should remain featured until a post with comparable traffic potential is published.

**Design Notes:**

*   The featured post is manually selected by the editorial team, not auto-populated by date. This allows Blue Tree to highlight their strongest content regardless of publication date.
*   The author attribution with headshot and title is a mandatory E-E-A-T signal — not optional design flair.
*   Featured post should be swappable via CMS without requiring a code deployment.
