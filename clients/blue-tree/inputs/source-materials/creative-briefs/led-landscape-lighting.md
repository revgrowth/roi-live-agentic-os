**How much does professional landscape lighting cost in Southeastern PA?**
Landscape lighting costs vary based on the number of fixtures, the size of the property, and the complexity of the design. **How long does a landscape lighting installation take?**
Most residential lighting installations are completed within two to five days, depending on the number of fixtures and the complexity of the wiring runs. If conduits were pre-installed during a previous landscape or hardscape project, installation time is significantly reduced. Your designer will provide a detailed timeline during the proposal process.

**What brand of landscape lighting fixtures do you use?**
We primarily specify Vista lighting fixtures—a commercial-grade LED brand known for durability, consistent color temperature, and long-term weather resistance. **Can I control my landscape lighting from my phone?**
Yes. We install Wi-Fi-enabled smart controllers that allow you to set schedules, adjust lighting zones, and turn the system on or off from your phone—anywhere, anytime. You can program lights to turn on at dusk and off at any designated hour, adjust for seasonal daylight changes, and control individual zones independently.

**Do LED landscape lights use a lot of electricity?**
No. LED landscape lighting is dramatically more energy-efficient than traditional halogen systems. LED fixtures consume a fraction of the wattage, produce less heat, and last tens of thousands of hours before needing replacement. Most homeowners find the monthly operating cost to be minimal.

**Can you add lighting to an existing landscape without tearing everything up?**
Absolutely. Our installation crews are experienced at running low-voltage wiring through existing landscapes with minimal disruption to beds, walkways, and turf.
