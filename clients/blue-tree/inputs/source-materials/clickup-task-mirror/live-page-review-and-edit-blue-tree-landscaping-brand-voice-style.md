# Review and edit Blue Tree Landscaping Brand Voice & Style

The ROI.LIVE Brand Voice "Brain Dump" Cheat Sheet
Instructions: Do not write a script. Do not overthink this. Just open the voice memo app on your phone (or a Loom video) and talk to me like we are having a coffee. I need the raw, unpolished you.
Please record a separate short clip for these 4 sections:
1. The Service Pitches (The Money Makers)
Please do this for EVERY major service you offer (e.g., Service A, Service B, Service C).
Part A: The "Foot in the Door" (Phone): Pretend I am a skeptical lead on the phone. What do you say to convince me to book the appointment/estimate? How do you get your foot in the door?
Part B: The "Kitchen Table" Close (In-Person): You are now in my home or office. You've inspected the problem or project. Give me your best pitch. Walk me through the solution and why I should sign the contract right now rather than waiting.
2. The Brand Story (The Soul)
Tell me the story of your business from start to finish:
Origins: Why did you start this? What was the struggle in the beginning?
Evolution: How have you changed from day one to today?
The "Big Why": Why did you build this (beyond just money)? What drives you?
Legacy & Impact: How does your business impact the local community? What legacy are you leaving behind?
3. The "Objection Crusher" (The Defense)
What are the top 3 reasons people tell you "No" (e.g., Price, Timing, "I need to talk to my spouse")?
Record exactly how you answer those objections to turn that "No" into a "Yes."
4. The Ideal Client vs. The Nightmare
The Dream: Describe your absolute favorite customer. Who are they?
The Nightmare: Who do you never want to work with again? What are the red flags? (This helps us teach the AI who to avoid).
5. Customer Service FAQs
Prospects: What are the most commonly asked questions people ask before doing business with you and how do you answer them?
Clients: What questions do active or past clients ask you? Warranties, returns, etc.
