**Attendees:** Jason Spencer, Jérôme Besnard, Blue Tree Landscaping, Inc.
### Overview

The meeting focused on reviewing detailed marketing performance metrics and discussing strategic adjustments to budgets and operational processes. The team aligned on transitioning to a new web‑based ERP (Include) and initiating a comprehensive website redesign to boost organic search performance and streamline CRM functions.

### Key Takeaways

*   The team decided to transition from Single Ops to the new web‑based ERP system, Include, to streamline CRM operations and data migration.
*   They agreed to reallocate the November ad budget by reducing Facebook spend to $500 for pool leads, allocating $2,000 toward Google Ads for pools, $1,000 for landscaping, and increasing investment in SEO backlinks to $1,500.
*   The team committed to a website redesign using a user‑friendly WordPress CMS with streamlined navigation, supported by a design process that includes multiple mockups and 0% financing over five months.
*   They endorsed a simplified promotional strategy for November focusing on a single offer (10% off on lighting with a $5,000 landscaping spend) to avoid customer confusion and enhance ROI.
*   The next meeting was scheduled for next week at 10:00 to review the website agenda, updated budgets, and design concepts.

### Key Topics
*   **ERP and Data/CRM Transition** Update
    *   Jérôme explained that the company had recently let go of a sales representative, which prompted a closer look at their operational and data management systems.
    *   Discussion focused on phasing out the current Single Ops system and transitioning to a new, web-based ERP called 'Include'.
    *   They addressed challenges with data migration, particularly in transferring customer data, quotes, and sales metrics from Single Ops to the new system.
    *   The goal was to simplify processes and reduce manual work by improving CRM automation and data accuracy.
    
*   **Marketing Channel Performance Analysis** Update
    *   Jason presented detailed performance metrics for SEO Organic, Google Ads, Meta Ads, and Klaviyo email/SMS campaigns.
    *   For SEO Organic, a strong conversion rate was highlighted, with notable figures such as a 51.41% quotes-to-revenue close rate and a low cost per lead, producing high ROI.
    *   Google Ads performance was reviewed, noting a seasonal peak in June, with a decreasing trend in ad spend and leads in subsequent months. ROAS figures showed robust returns despite lower overall revenue compared to SEO.
    *   Meta Ads were analyzed, and while they showed lower conversion rates and higher cost per lead, the discussion emphasized using them strategically during the peak season rather than continuous heavy spending.
    *   Overall campaign metrics were discussed including lead volumes, appointments, close rates, and revenue per channel, illustrating the strengths and opportunities in each channel.
    
*   **Budget Reallocation and Promotional Strategy for November** Decision
    *   The team discussed recalibrating ad spend for the off-season, including reducing Facebook ad spend to $500, focusing primarily on pool leads.
    *   Google Ads spend was proposed to be split with approximately $2,000 allocated toward pool campaigns and $1,000 toward landscaping/hardscaping, to match seasonal demand.
    *   An increase in investment toward paid backlinks was suggested, with a target budget of $1,500, to further enhance SEO performance during slower months.
    *   A simplified promotional strategy was proposed, centering on a single offer (e.g., 10% off on lighting when spending $5,000 on landscaping) to reduce customer confusion and streamline messaging.
    *   Final approval from Jeff was noted as a necessary next step before these budget adjustments could be implemented.
    
*   **Website Redesign and CMS Upgrade** Decision
    *   There was an extensive discussion on the need to revamp the existing website, with an emphasis on improving user experience, mobile navigation, and conversion.
    *   Jason described the design process, which includes creating 2-3 design concepts from his design team based on client references and feedback, followed by iterative revisions.
    *   The new website is planned to be built on WordPress using user-friendly page builders (such as Elementor or Breakdance) with a one-time CMS license fee and no recurring CMS charges.
    *   A project management approach via ClickUp was discussed to ensure timely reviews, approvals, and smooth transition from design to development.
    *   Financing options were offered, including splitting payments 0% interest over several months, to ease the cost burden for the client.
    
*   **SEO and Backlink Strategy Optimization** Decision
    *   Alongside organic SEO efforts, the team emphasized the importance of investing in quality backlinks to further boost the website's search ranking.
    *   There was a consensus to explore reallocating part of the advertising budget away from paid channels, particularly reducing Facebook spend, in order to scale up investment in backlinks.
    *   This strategy was seen as crucial for long-term organic growth and maximizing ROI during slower lead-generating periods.
    
*   **Integration of Services and Operational Scheduling Insights** Update
    *   The discussion noted that pool leads often lead to additional projects in landscaping and hardscaping, adding complexity in scheduling and permitting.
    *   Seasonal variations were discussed, with pool projects sometimes delayed due to permitting issues and weather, which in turn affects scheduling for additional services.
    *   The team recognized the need to better align marketing efforts with operational capacities to ensure crews are optimally scheduled even during slower months.
    
*   **Next Steps and Action Items** Update
    *   Jason agreed to send a detailed summary email including SEO traffic reports, performance metrics, and the website redesign process, copying Jeff and relevant team members.
    *   Jérôme planned to draft an email outlining the proposed ad budget reallocation and promotional strategy for November, pending confirmation from Jeff.
    *   They agreed to gather design references and ideas to steer the new website's layout, navigation, and overall aesthetic.
    *   A follow-up meeting was scheduled for next week (tentatively at 10:00) to review budget updates, website design concepts, and coordinate next steps on the ERP and advertising strategies.
