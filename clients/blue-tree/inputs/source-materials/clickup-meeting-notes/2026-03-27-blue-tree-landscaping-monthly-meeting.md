    *   Jason explained the enhancements made to the marketing dashboard by integrating AI and multiple data sources including the CRM, Google Ads, and Facebook. This integration enabled granular tracking of metrics such as lead tracking, revenue matching, and quote matching.

    *   Significant delays and frustrations were discussed regarding the new website design, particularly focusing on color palette issues and layout inconsistencies (e.g., excessive use of orange, misaligned text, and improper placement of elements).
    *   Maureen voiced concerns about the current design, emphasizing the need for a professional, cohesive look that aligns with the brand’s approved color scheme derived from the logo.
    *   It was agreed that the homepage must be finalized first, with multiple design samples (using variations in the palette) to be reviewed and approved before moving into the development phase of core pages.
    *   Jason mentioned that once the design is developed in WordPress, making changes such as color adjustments will be easier in the live environment.
    
*   **Operational and Growth Considerations** Idea
    *   Jason highlighted the company's potential for significant growth if marketing spend increases and operational capacity (sales teams and production) can scale accordingly.
    *   There was discussion about differentiating between different types of pool leads (e.g., new construction vs. renovations) to ensure that quality leads are targeted and to make strategic decisions based on lead quality.
    *   The conversation emphasized that increasing ad spend is not the limitation; rather, it’s the organization’s operational capacity to handle the potential volume of new leads.
    
*   **Communication and Coordination in the Design Process** Problem
    *   The meeting addressed gaps in communication regarding design feedback, noting that critical comments (especially about color choices and layout issues) were not being relayed in real-time.
    *   Jason committed to stepping in and coordinating more closely with the design team to resolve these issues, including offering multiple homepage design options that reflect various color palettes.
    *   It was acknowledged that once the website moves into the development phase, clients like Maureen will have the flexibility to make fine-tuned adjustments directly, reducing dependency on the design team for color changes.
    
*   **Next Steps and Action Items** Decision
    *   It was agreed to closely monitor the performance of the updated ad spend strategy for the next four weeks, with potential further adjustments based on lead quality and operational capacity.
    *   The team decided to focus on finalizing the homepage design as a critical step before developing additional core pages for the website launch.
    *   Jason committed to work directly with the design team to provide revised design options and to ensure that the approved design meets the brand’s aesthetics and functional requirements.
    *   Future integration plans, including connecting the CRM and ERP via API, were mentioned to facilitate more real-time data tracking and decision-making.
