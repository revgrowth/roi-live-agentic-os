### Key Topics
*   **Recurring Strategy Meetings** Decision
    *   The team agreed to schedule recurring monthly strategy calls to review past performance and plan for the upcoming month.
    *   Meetings were tentatively set for Tuesdays at 10 am on the second or third Tuesday of each month.
    *   Jason was assigned to send out calendar invites and set up the meeting links using Google Meet.
    
*   **Document and File Management Integration** Update
    *   Discussions focused on integrating Google Drive and Teams for storing meeting agendas, marketing files, and creative assets.
    *   Jérôme created a folder in Teams for agendas and meeting documents with links to video recordings, while Maureen manages content uploads.
    *   The team explored using Zapier to automate file transfers so that files are automatically copied to Jason’s designated Google Drive folder.
    
*   **Website Forms, CRM Integration, and Lead Automation** Update
    *   Jason provided updates on the website forms: there are two versions (one for cold traffic and one on the main website) that feed into the CRM.
    *   The lead capture process was discussed, highlighting automation via Zapier to populate fields such as budget, timeline, and service interests.
    *   Jérôme noted occasional issues with missing fields (e.g. first name, last name, zip code) and mentioned follow-up improvements including faster email outreach for leads.
    
*   **Facebook Leads Performance and Advertising Strategy** Problem
    *   The team reviewed performance metrics, noting that Facebook Instant Form leads have a low response rate (~20%) compared to other channels.
    *   There was discussion on potentially reallocating budget away from Facebook Instant forms towards more effective channels such as Google Ads or website forms.
    *   Jason mentioned reviewing data and scaling the ad spend based on lead quality and ROI.
    
*   **Email Drip Campaigns for Lost/Abandoned Leads** Idea
    *   Jérôme proposed creating a six-email drip campaign specifically for leads that are lost or abandoned in the CRM.
    *   This drip campaign is intended to nurture leads further, even if initial contact did not convert, ensuring continuous engagement.
    
*   **Website Redesign and Rebranding Considerations** Idea
    *   The team discussed the potential for a website redesign to improve SEO, conversion, and overall user experience.
    *   Rebranding considerations were raised, including updating the logo and potentially changing the URL, although concerns about high costs and SEO dip were noted.
    *   Jason was tasked with drafting a proposal that outlines the redesign strategy, cost structure, and technical improvements, while maintaining current brand recognition online.
    
*   **Social Media and Campaign Planning** Update
    *   Maureen and the team reviewed the strategy for organic social media, particularly for August and September campaign planning.
    *   The process was outlined to submit content ideas by the first week of August, allowing for proper inclusion in strategy documents and creative development.
    *   Discussions also touched on scheduling ad campaigns for pools, landscaping, and hardscaping based on seasonal factors.
    
*   **SEO and Backlink Strategy** Idea
    *   Jason emphasized the importance of scaling organic SEO efforts, including increasing backlink investment to expand service reach and drive higher quality leads.
    *   Discussion highlighted that organic traffic has been a major contributor to lead generation, warranting further investment in SEO initiatives.
    *   It was noted that improving SEO could allow the business to rank for a wider coverage area, ultimately increasing ROI over time.
