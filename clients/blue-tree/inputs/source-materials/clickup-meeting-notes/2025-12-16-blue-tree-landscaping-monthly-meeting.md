### Overview

The team aligned on digital marketing strategies, promotional campaign scheduling, and website rebranding initiatives to enhance conversion and brand consistency for Blue Tree Landscaping, Inc. The discussion focused on using ClickUp for task management, structuring focused promotional offers, and upgrading the website to improve SEO and user experience.

### Key Takeaways

*   The team agreed to enhance ClickUp usage with additional training and streamlined task management.
*   They decided to run separate, focused promotional offers rather than mixing multiple messages to avoid customer confusion.
*   A strategic plan was set for a website redesign and rebranding, including discussions on costs, financing, timelines, and the need for a fresher, faster site for improved conversion.
*   The budget allocation was reviewed with a plan to balance evergreen ads against high-impact promo offers, ensuring optimized spending.
*   Integrating brand voice into all digital content and improving system integration (e.g., transferring assets via Zapier and consolidating proposal systems) were highlighted as priorities.

### Key Topics
*   **ClickUp Training and Usage** Update
    *   The team discussed current challenges using ClickUp, including difficulties with viewing and editing old posts and the need for a calendar view.
    *   Jason mentioned that they intended to limit initial exposure to ClickUp to task creation and basic conversation features, with further training and expansion planned.
    *   Participants agreed that additional ClickUp training is needed to streamline task management and ensure team members like Maureen can effectively update content.
    
*   **Promotional Offer Structure and Scheduling** Decision
    *   The discussion covered the process for setting up digital campaigns, including deadlines for ad and social content (e.g., completing 10 posts and creative assets by the 24th of the month to allow for review).
    *   Detailed workflows were shared on integrating seasonal offer promotions (such as turf care and spring reseeding) with organic social posts and Klaviyo emails.
    *   The team debated whether to run multiple offers simultaneously or to focus on one at a time to avoid overwhelming potential customers.
    
*   **Ad Budget Allocation and Campaign Segmentation** Idea
    *   A proposed split of approximately 70% evergreen advertising and 30% promotional spend was discussed.
    *   The conversation involved setting up different buckets for offers: specific promos (like prepaid turf care at 5% off and spring reseeding at 10% off) versus evergreen campaigns for services such as pool, landscaping, hardscaping, and outdoor living.
    *   There was discussion on testing variables by running either concurrent promotions (with split budgets) or sequential promotions, with measurement of cost per acquisition and response volume.
    
*   **Website Redesign and Rebranding** Decision
    *   A significant portion of the meeting was dedicated to the deficiencies of the current website, including issues with speed, outdated design, and negative impacts on SEO and conversion rates.
    *   Jason presented Figma design proposals and discussed different website design options, including a full redesign estimated around $19,000 and a separate rebranding option (new logo) estimated at $2,500.
    *   Financing options were discussed (e.g., a down payment and monthly installments), with a tentative launch target by end February or early March, emphasizing the need for an improved user experience to maximize conversion.
    
*   **Integration of Systems and Process Automation** Update
    *   The team mentioned challenges with the current proposal system and noted that proposals would continue to be delivered from the old system until the new system is fully integrated.
    *   There was a discussion on integrating QuickBooks with Include in the longer term, while in the interim, QuickBooks would remain active.
    *   The possibility of using Zapier to transfer images from Teams to ClickUp was raised to streamline asset management.
    
*   **Brand Voice and Content Strategy** Update
    *   Jason emphasized the development of a brand voice document to capture the authentic language of the sales team, ensuring consistency across website copy, social media posts, and ad campaigns.
    *   A new blog post series focused on Turf Care was mentioned, serving as an authority hub for the service area and integrating town/city prioritization based on factors like proximity and demographics.
    *   The team discussed leveraging recorded sales pitches and transcripts to refine content, ensuring that AI-generated copy aligns with the company’s authentic voice.
    
*   **Operational Coordination and Next Steps** Decision
    *   Action items included scheduling a dedicated ClickUp training session, reviewing the brand voice document and town prioritization scoring system, and finalizing promotional schedules.
    *   Maureen and Jeff were tasked with reviewing and providing feedback on creative documents and promotional offers, while Jason committed to circulating design proposals for the website rebrand.
    *   The team agreed to evaluate budget allocations for upcoming campaigns and consider system integrations to streamline data transfer and asset management, ensuring smooth operational execution moving forward.
