### Overview

The meeting focused on reviewing the marketing performance across SEO, Google Ads, and Meta while addressing creative execution and website design challenges for Blue Tree. The team discussed budget misallocations, seasonal adjustments, and rebranding ideas to optimize campaign effectiveness during a slower economic period.

### Key Takeaways

*   The team agreed to pause Meta ad campaigns for November and December, with plans to resume in mid-January.
*   They decided to reduce Google Ads spending to a baseline of $500 monthly while maintaining a backlink spend of $1,500.
*   Jason acknowledged the budget error and committed a $1500 credit on the next bill to correct the misallocation between Google and Facebook ads.
*   The decision was made to remove Busola from the creative process, with Jason taking direct responsibility for improving creative outputs and website content.
*   The group planned to present new homepage design concepts and a rebranding proposal to better reflect Blue Tree’s full range of services.
*   The team discussed shifting mass email marketing to Klaviyo for improved deliverability and also addressed plans for ADA compliance and updating local citations on the website.

### Key Topics
*   **Logistical Coordination and Meeting Link Issues**
    *   The meeting began with challenges related to joining the virtual call, including issues with sharing and accessing the calendar link via email and Teams.
    *   Participants had minor delays in connecting, highlighting the need for smoother communication across platforms.
    
*   **Performance Metrics Review and Budget Correction** Update
    *   Jason presented detailed performance metrics across marketing channels including SEO/Organic, Google Ads, and Meta (Facebook) Ads, citing figures on quotes, revenue, leads, appointments, and conversion rates.
    *   A misallocation was identified where the budgets for Google Ads and Facebook Ads were switched; Jason acknowledged the error and offered a $1500 credit on the next bill.
    *   Discussions covered month-to-month variability in return on ad spend and lead-to-sale conversion rates, with insights into seasonal impacts on performance.
    
*   **Budget Adjustments and Seasonal Strategy** Decision
    *   The team discussed reducing ad spend during slower months (November and December) by pausing Meta campaigns and lowering Google Ads spend to a baseline of $500.
    *   They agreed to maintain the backlink expenditure at $1,500 to support SEO efforts, essential for a strong ramp-up in February and March.
    *   Jeff and team provided insights on seasonal trends, noting that December and January are typically slower in sales.
    
*   **Email Marketing Platforms and Domain Reputation Management** Update
    *   The use of High Level for sending mass emails was reviewed, with concerns raised about email deliverability and high spam rates.
    *   The discussion included the possibility of switching to Klaviyo (or evaluating Constant Contact) for better inbox placement and improved domain reputation.
    *   Jason recommended Klaviyo for its superior performance and offered to assist in building out email campaigns to better target specific groups.
    
*   **Creative and Website Design Concerns** Decision
    *   Client representatives, particularly Maureen and Jérôme, expressed dissatisfaction with the creative assets provided, noting that the work from Busola had recurring errors and lacked engaging design.
    *   Concerns were raised that the creative output did not effectively represent the full range of the company’s services beyond pools.
    *   Jason took responsibility for these issues, announcing that he would remove Busola from the process and personally oversee all creative work going forward, ensuring improvements on ad design, social content, and promotional material.
    *   Discussions also touched on potential rebranding, including a new logo and a unified aesthetic for the website and marketing collateral.
    
*   **Website Enhancements and ADA Compliance** Update
    *   The conversation included plans for updating the website, with emphasis on improving the front-end creative design to better align with the company’s brand and broader service offering.
    *   There were discussions about re-enabling ADA compliance and updating local citations (counties) to enhance accessibility and local SEO, with Jason set to provide cost details.
    *   Participants noted the importance of integrating these improvements with ongoing SEO initiatives and future rebranding efforts.
    
*   **Next Steps and Action Items** Decision
    *   Immediate actions include applying a $1500 credit to correct the budget misallocation, pausing Meta ad campaigns for the remainder of November and December, and reducing Google Ads spend to $500 monthly while maintaining $1,500 for backlinks.
    *   Jason committed to developing and presenting three new homepage design concepts and to managing creative outputs personally to address previous deficiencies.
    *   Plans were made to sync email marketing efforts with Klaviyo for better deliverability, and follow-up communication will address website improvements (ADA compliance and local citations) and rebranding considerations.
    *   The team agreed to reconvene next month to review the implemented adjustments and creative revisions.
