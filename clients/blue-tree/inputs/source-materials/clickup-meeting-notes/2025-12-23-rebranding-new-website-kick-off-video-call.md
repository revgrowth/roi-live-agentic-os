### Overview

The meeting focused on initiating the rebranding and new website strategy, with an emphasis on evolving the legacy blue tree logo by incorporating modern elements like a blue wave and accent colors. Discussions addressed tagline options, website design mockups, and the process for developing an authentic brand voice to drive future copy development.

### Key Takeaways

*   The team agreed to evolve the current logo by retaining the blue tree and exploring design variations, including separating the text from the image and integrating a blue wave element with accent orange.
*   Participants leaned toward a tagline that emphasizes the pool service aspect (e.g., “Pools, Landscapes”) to clearly communicate their offerings and support SEO efforts.
*   Figma Sample Two was selected as the baseline for the new website design, appreciated for its modern, app-like aesthetic with rounded corners and intuitive navigation.
*   A phased approach was established: starting with brand voice recordings to inform website copy, followed by iterative logo and website design refinements.
*   A ClickUp training session was scheduled to streamline project management and task delegation.

### Key Topics
*   **Rebranding Strategy & Logo Evolution** Decision
    *   The meeting was primarily focused on evolving the current company logo while retaining key legacy elements, specifically the blue tree, to maintain brand recognition.
    *   Discussions included incorporating a blue wave element, exploring the use of accent orange, and experimenting with both integrated and separated arrangements of the logo text and image.
    *   Jeff emphasized that the blue tree must remain unchanged given its importance, while Jerome and Jason proposed modernizing the design with updated fonts, spacing, and layout adjustments.
    *   The team agreed to produce multiple logo mockups over a three to four week period, with some designs showing the image and text separated to enhance clarity.
    
*   **Tagline Discussion & Messaging Clarity** Decision
    *   The team evaluated different tagline options to effectively communicate the company’s expanded service offerings.
    *   There was a debate between a more descriptive, SEO-friendly tagline such as “Pools, Landscapes” (or a variation like “Pools and Landscapes”) versus a broader term like “Outdoor Living” that connotes luxury and a lifestyle approach.
    *   Jason, Jeff, and Maureen discussed that while a literal tagline might benefit SEO by clearly indicating services (especially pools), the broader option might better reflect the company’s modern, comprehensive approach.
    *   The discussion concluded with leaning towards emphasizing pools in the tagline to differentiate the company as a pool builder that also offers landscaping, though final decisions would be made after reviewing logo mockups.
    
*   **Website Design & Layout Considerations** Decision
    *   The team reviewed various website design concepts presented in Figma, with a specific preference for Sample Two due to its modern, app-like aesthetic and rounded corners.
    *   Discussions focused on key navigation elements such as a fixed top alert/banner, clear call-to-action (CTA) sections, and an effective footer (with reference to design elements from Sample Six).
    *   There was an emphasis on ensuring that the website design would be both visually appealing and functional for various devices, with considerations for menu structures like a hamburger menu for mobile.
    *   Jason highlighted that the chosen design would serve as a baseline template that could be fully customized based on further feedback, especially after the brand voice and copy are finalized.
    
*   **Integration of AI in the Creative Process** Update
    *   Jason explained the use of AI, specifically Gemini Ultra and custom-built agents, in generating ideas for the brand voice and tagline options.
    *   The AI analysis provided insights on SEO impact for different taglines, recommending options that explicitly stated pool building for better market positioning.
    *   This innovative approach aimed to merge technology with creative design, supporting data-driven decisions in the rebranding process.
    
*   **Phased Approach: Brand Voice, Copy & Rebranding Implementation** Update
    *   The rebranding process was outlined as a phased approach: establishing a clear brand voice through team recordings, developing website copy based on that voice, and then integrating the copy with the website design.
    *   It was agreed that voice recordings would be collected from various team members (including in-home client interactions) to ensure authentic messaging.
    *   Jason indicated that he would provide a detailed list of recording prompts and requirements following the meeting to facilitate the brand voice development.
    *   The brand voice would directly influence the website copy, ensuring consistency between how the company communicates verbally and on its website.
    
*   **Next Steps & Action Items** Decision
    *   Jason committed to sharing a detailed list of recording prompts for creating the brand voice and compiling a list of service pages that should be featured on the website.
    *   The creative team is expected to start working on logo mockups based on the agreed direction, with multiple concepts (both integrated and separated versions) to be reviewed.
    *   Jeff requested a definitive list of services and page structure to delegate responsibilities for website content, ensuring standalone pages for key services like pool installation, maintenance, and renovation.
    *   A ClickUp training session was scheduled for January 29th at 8:00 AM to streamline project management and task assignments related to the rebranding initiative.
    
*   **Coordination & Scheduling Considerations** Update
    *   Timeline challenges were addressed, noting that the new ERP implementation is concurrent with the rebranding project and may affect some deadlines.
    *   It was suggested that the brand voice recordings be completed by around January 9th, with continued iterations on design and copy following that milestone.
    *   The team coordinated specific timing for ClickUp training and other collaborative sessions, emphasizing the need for early morning meetings to suit scheduling constraints.
