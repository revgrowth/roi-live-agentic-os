### Overview

The meeting focused on reviewing the exceptional digital marketing performance, especially the impressive ROI from pool service ads, and discussed strategies to scale ad spend and improve lead generation. The team also explored website enhancements and dynamic form integrations to streamline the consultation process and better integrate with their CRM.

### Key Takeaways

*   The team agreed to boost pool lead generation by increasing ad spend—with initial discussions around a test spend of $5,000 leading to an overall plan to target 48 pool sales through cost-effective campaigns.
*   The group decided to launch meta campaigns alongside existing Google campaigns to quickly ramp up pool leads while preparing to shift towards more organic traffic as the website and SEO improvements take effect.
*   It was determined to enhance the website user experience by incorporating a dynamic, slide-out consultation form that features quick pick options and an updated instructions page organized into pre-, during, and post-service sections.
*   The team consolidated the design feedback discussion and agreed to work solely in the “Design Final, Phase 1” Figma file to ensure all comments on aspects like the appointment slider and instructions layout are addressed.

*   **Balancing Paid Ads and Organic Growth** Update
    *   There was discussion on leveraging the exceptional ROI from paid ads while planning for a transition to more organic lead generation through website and SEO improvements.
    *   Jason noted that once the new website is live and organic leads increase, the reliance on high paid ad spend might decrease.
    
*   **Enhancements to Website and CRM Integration** Idea
    *   The conversation turned to website functionality improvements, including the development of an instructions page outlining pre-, during-, and post-service details to set customer expectations.
    *   A proposal was made to incorporate dynamic, multi-step forms (using slide-out or pop-up interfaces) that adapt based on user selections (e.g., pools, landscaping, hardscaping) to simplify lead capture.
    *   These dynamic forms were discussed in the context of integrating with the CRM for automated emails and enhanced customer communication.
    *   Exit pop-ups were also mentioned as a potential tool to capture additional leads and highlight the full range of services.
    
*   **Figma Design File Clarification and Coordination** Update
    *   Maureen raised concerns about which Figma file version to use, specifically debating between 'Design Final, Phase 1' and other versions.
    *   It was agreed that feedback should be consolidated into the 'Design Final, Phase 1' file, ensuring that comments on the homepage, service page, blog, and other pages are unified before moving to development.
    *   The discussion underscored the importance of clear version control and coordination between design and development teams.
    
*   **Immediate Next Steps and Action Items** Decision
    *   Jason committed to performing a deep dive into ad spend data, specifically breaking down cost per lead and cost per pool sale, and to follow up with an email later the same day.
    *   There was consensus on activating and scaling both Meta and Google campaigns as soon as possible to boost pool lead generation.
    *   Agreements were made to modify the website’s contact experience, including transitioning the quote/appointment process to a dynamic slide-out form.
    *   The team planned to reconvene in a couple of weeks to review lead volume performance and adjust strategies as needed.
