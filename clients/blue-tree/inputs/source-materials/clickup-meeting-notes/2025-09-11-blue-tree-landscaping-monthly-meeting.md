### Overview

The meeting reviewed the company’s recent marketing performance across organic channels, Google Ads, and Meta, focusing on detailed metrics such as leads, appointments, sales, and revenue. The group addressed challenges with the current reporting systems and discussed strategic budget reallocation and a comprehensive website revamp to enhance SEO, mobile responsiveness, and overall user experience.

### Key Takeaways

*   The team agreed to increase the Google Ads budget to approximately $6,000 for September while reducing the Meta budget to about $1,000 per month.
*   A complete website revamp was prioritized to replace the outdated system with improved SEO, mobile optimization, and easier front-end management; a proposal with design options and payment plans will be developed.
*   The team decided to refine the email campaign strategy by segmenting the audience to boost open and click-through rates.
*   Reporting challenges due to discrepancies in the existing CRM and accounting systems were recognized as a barrier to accurate decision-making.
*   Technical follow-ups were identified, including implementing a new pop-up integration using Zap and exploring a custom URL note feature for enhanced lead management.

### Key Topics
*   **Marketing Performance Metrics Analysis** Update
    *   Jason Spencer presented detailed monthly data on quotes, leads, appointments, and conversion rates, showing variations across April to August.
    *   He highlighted strong conversion rates from appointments to sales and a high overall lead-to-sale conversion percentage compared to industry averages.
    *   Emphasis was placed on the impressive return on ad spend, particularly noting the performance of the organic channel.
    
*   **Reporting System Challenges and Data Discrepancies** Problem
    *   The team discussed significant discrepancies between front-end Excel records and back-end accounting systems (Single Ops/QuickBooks).
    *   Concerns were raised about delays in revenue data entry and the inability to capture add-on revenue from existing contracts.
    *   There was agreement on the need for more accurate and timely reporting to aid decision-making.
    
*   **Channel-Specific Performance and Strategy Adjustments** Decision
    *   Organic/SEO demonstrated strong performance with a high return on ad spend, with detailed figures showing robust revenue generation from organic leads.
    *   Google Ads performance was reviewed, noting decent conversion figures but seasonal fluctuations. The plan is to increase the budget to $6,000 in September.
    *   Meta (Facebook Ads) underperformed considerably, prompting a decision to reduce its budget from approximately $4,000 to $1,000 per month.
    *   Discussion included the role of social media organic content in supporting brand presence, though it was not seen as a major lead generation channel.
    
*   **Email Campaign Strategy Adjustments** Idea
    *   The team reviewed current email campaign performance, noting low click-through rates possibly affected by outdated contact lists.
    *   A strategy was proposed to send initial emails to the full list, followed by targeted follow-ups to engaged users to boost open rates and engagement.
    
*   **Website Revamp and Rebranding Plans** Idea
    *   The outdated website structure was identified as a major barrier for quick updates and optimal SEO performance.
    *   Jason proposed a complete overhaul of the website, likening it to rebuilding a failing structure, to improve mobile usability and ease of content changes.
    *   The revamp is intended to serve as the foundation for future rebranding efforts, with the update including design drafts, modern page builders, and clear payment plans.
    *   There was discussion on maintaining current domain names while changing visual elements like logos and color schemes in the rebranding process.
    
*   **Ad Budget and Campaign Duration Adjustments** Decision
    *   The team agreed to recalibrate ad spending: increasing Google Ads to $6,000 and reducing Meta spend to around $1,000 per month.
    *   They discussed extending promotional campaigns beyond one month to optimize lead generation and conversion, with potential new promotions for October.
    *   Jason suggested that adjustments take into account seasonal trends, with decreased spending during off-peak months and ramping up before the peak season.
    
*   **Follow-Up Items and Action Planning** Update
    *   Jason Spencer committed to delivering a detailed website revamp quote with design options and payment plan proposals before the next meeting on October 2.
