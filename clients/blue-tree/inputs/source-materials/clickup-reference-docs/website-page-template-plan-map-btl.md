### \[FAQ SECTION\]
**Section Headline:** Landscape Design Questions Answered
**FAQs (6):**

| Question | Answer |
| ---| --- |
| How much does landscape design cost in Pennsylvania? | Landscape costs vary significantly based on property size, scope, and plant selections. A foundation refresh might start around $5,000-$15,000, while comprehensive property landscaping can range from $25,000 to $100,000+. We provide detailed proposals with transparent pricing after our site consultation—no surprises. |
| What's the difference between a landscaper and a landscape designer? | A landscaper typically focuses on installation and maintenance. A landscape designer creates the plan—analyzing your site, developing a cohesive design, specifying appropriate plants, and producing drawings. At Blue Tree, our designers are degreed professionals who both design AND oversee installation. |
| How long does a landscape project take? | Timeline depends on project scope. A foundation planting might take 2-3 days to install. A full property landscape could take 2-4 weeks. Design development typically takes 2-3 weeks before installation begins. We'll provide a clear timeline in your proposal. |
| When is the best time to plant in Southeastern PA? | Spring (April-May) and fall (September-October) are ideal planting seasons in our region. Fall is actually preferred for many plants—cooler temperatures and fall rains help roots establish before winter dormancy. We can design anytime and schedule installation for optimal planting windows. |
| Do you offer a warranty on plants? | Yes. We provide a one-year, one-time replacement warranty on all plant material from the date of installation. If a plant fails due to conditions beyond your control (extreme weather, disease), we'll replace it. The warranty doesn't cover plants that die from improper watering—but we'll give you clear care instructions to prevent that. |
| What if I only have a partial budget right now? | We design for phased implementation all the time. We can create a master plan for your entire property, then prioritize what gets installed first based on your budget and needs. Many clients start with the front yard or a specific problem area, then add phases over subsequent years. |
