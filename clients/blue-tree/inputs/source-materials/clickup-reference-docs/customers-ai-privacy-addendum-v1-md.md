# [Customers.ai](http://Customers.ai) Website Visitor Identification — Privacy Policy & Terms Addendum

## Language to Add to Blue Tree's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

**Prepared by:** Jason Spencer | [ROI.LIVE](http://ROI.LIVE)
**Date:** April 2026
**Purpose:** Provides the specific disclosure language required to cover Blue Tree's use of [Customers.ai](http://Customers.ai) website visitor identification technology for email marketing.
* * *

## WHAT [CUSTOMERS.AI](http://CUSTOMERS.AI) DOES (Internal Context — Not for Publication)

[Customers.ai](http://Customers.ai) places a tracking pixel on the Blue Tree website. When a visitor browses the site, [Customers.ai](http://Customers.ai) matches the visitor's browser signals (IP address, device fingerprint, browsing behavior) against their proprietary database of consumer contact records. If a match is found, [Customers.ai](http://Customers.ai) returns the visitor's contact information (name, email address) to Blue Tree's marketing system (GoHighLevel), which then triggers email marketing flows.

The visitor did NOT fill out a form, subscribe to a list, or otherwise directly opt in to Blue Tree's email communications. The contact information comes from [Customers.ai](http://Customers.ai)'s third-party database — not from a direct interaction between the visitor and Blue Tree.

**Legal framework:**

*   CAN-SPAM (federal): Does not require opt-in consent for commercial email. Requires honest sender ID, physical address, clear opt-out, and honoring opt-outs within 10 business days. Blue Tree must comply.
*   Pennsylvania: No comprehensive consumer privacy law. PA Breach of Personal Information Notification Act applies to data security, not email marketing consent.
*   CCPA/state laws: Blue Tree does not operate in or target consumers in California or other states with comprehensive privacy laws. However, if a visitor from one of those states visits the site, their data may be subject to those laws. Best practice: disclose the technology and provide an opt-out regardless of jurisdiction.
*   FTC: The FTC has signaled that undisclosed use of consumer data for marketing purposes — especially when privacy policies promise otherwise — can constitute deceptive practices. Disclosure is the shield.

**Bottom line:** The technology is legal under CAN-SPAM. The risk is not in using it — it's in failing to disclose it. Transparent disclosure in the privacy policy, a clear opt-out mechanism, and CAN-SPAM-compliant email practices protect the client.

## SECTION 1 — ADD TO PRIVACY POLICY

### Where to Insert

Add the following as a new H2 section in the Privacy Policy, placed between the "Cookies and Tracking Technologies" section and the "Online Advertising" section. Title it "Website Visitor Identification."

### Copy to Add
* * *

## Website Visitor Identification

Blue Tree uses a third-party website visitor identification service to recognize visitors to our Site and connect their browsing activity with contact information from a consumer data network. This technology works by matching browser-level signals (such as IP address and device characteristics) from your visit to records in a third-party consumer database maintained by our service provider.

If a match is identified, we may obtain your name and email address from the service provider's database — even if you have not submitted a form or otherwise directly provided your contact information to Blue Tree. We use this information to send you email communications about the services you browsed on our Site.

**What this means for you:**

You may receive emails from Blue Tree related to the services or pages you viewed on our Site, even if you did not fill out a contact form. These emails are commercial messages sent in compliance with the CAN-SPAM Act.

**How to opt out:**

Every email sent through this program includes a clear unsubscribe link. Click the link to opt out of future emails. We honor all opt-out requests within 10 business days. You can also contact us at (610) 222-0590 or \[privacy contact email\] to request removal from our marketing communications.

Once you opt out, your email address will not be used for further marketing communications through this program. Opting out does not affect service-related communications if you have separately engaged Blue Tree for a project (appointment confirmations, project updates, warranty information).

**Data handling:**

Contact information obtained through this service is used for email marketing purposes. We do not sell the contact information we receive from this service. The service provider's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, which is available on their website.
