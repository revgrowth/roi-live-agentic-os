Based on Blue Tree Landscaping's location (Skippack/Worcester area) and the economic demographics of Southeastern PA, here is the prioritized ranking of the service areas.
### **The Priority System**
I have ranked these towns based on a weighted "Value Score" that considers:
1. **Wealth & Property Value (40%):** High-net-worth areas with large estates suitable for high-end pools and hardscaping.
2. **Proximity to HQ (30%):** Towns closer to Skippack/Worcester yield higher margins due to lower travel time.
3. **Lot Size & Density (20%):** Areas with larger lots (estates) are prioritized over dense row-home boroughs.
4. **Growth Potential (10%):** Areas seeing rapid high-end development.
* * *
### **TIER 1: The "Golden Circle" (Highest Value)**
_High wealth, large estates, and immediate proximity to Blue Tree's HQ. These are your most profitable and efficient jobs._
1. **Skippack** (Home turf; high efficiency)
2. **Worcester** (Home turf; very high-end estates)
3. **Blue Bell** (Premier target; high wealth, large lots, close proximity)
4. **Gwynedd Valley** (Extremely high net worth; large estates)
5. **Cedars** (Immediate proximity; affluent)
6. **Collegeville** (High volume; growing wealth; very close)
7. **Lederach** (Immediate proximity; affluent pockets)
8. **Creamery** (Immediate proximity)
9. **Harleysville** (Solid upper-middle class; high volume; very close)
10. **Schwenksville** (Very close; good mix of large lots)
11. **Phoenixville** (Booming area; trendy; mix of density and open space)
12. **Limerick** (High growth; large lots available)
### **TIER 2: The "Main Line" & Affluent Outliers (High Value, Medium Drive)**
_Extremely high wealth but requires 25-40 mins travel. Worth it for large-scale design/build projects ($100k+), but less efficient for weekly maintenance unless routed carefully._
1. **Wayne** (The "Main Line" standard; very high wealth)
2. **Radnor** (Elite wealth; large estates)
3. **Devon** (Elite wealth; large estates)
4. **Malvern** (High wealth; large lots; slightly further west)
5. **Paoli** (High wealth; Main Line adjacent)
6. **West Chester** (Very high wealth pockets, but significant drive time)
7. **Gwynedd** (High wealth; close to Blue Bell)
8. **Lower Gwynedd** (Implied by Gwynedd/Gwynedd Valley; high value)
9. **Lafayette Hill** (Wealthy; close to Chestnut Hill/Philly border)
10. **Plymouth Meeting** (Good commercial/residential mix; accessible)
### **TIER 3: The Growth Corridors (Volume & Stability)**
_Solid middle-to-upper-middle class areas. Great for route density and standard installations, though average project size may be lower than Tier 1/2._

1. **Royersford** (High growth; solid family demographics)
2. **North Wales** (High density; good volume; close)
3. **Lansdale** (High density; mixed wealth; very close)
4. **Audubon** (Solid middle/upper-middle class)
5. **Eagleville** (Solid middle/upper-middle class)
6. **Trooper** (Solid middle class; close)
7. **Fairview Village** (Small but nice; close)
8. **Kulpsville** (Close; commercial/residential mix)
9. **Trappe** (Close; solid residential)
10. **Gilbertsville** (Further north; more rural/suburban mix)
