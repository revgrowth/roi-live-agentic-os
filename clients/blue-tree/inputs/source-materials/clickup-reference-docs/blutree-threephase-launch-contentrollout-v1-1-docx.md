# **1\. Executive Summary**
The client has requested the redesigned site launch with core pages only, matching the approximate scope of the current [bluetreelandscaping.com](http://bluetreelandscaping.com). This document defines a three-phase rollout that gets the new site live faster while preserving the full 200-page architecture from the Sitemap & Implementation Plan v2.1.
**Phase 0 (Quick Launch):** 21 pages. Redesigned versions of every page that exists on the current site today, reorganized into the v2.1 five-pillar architecture. No cluster pages, no location pages, no blog posts.
**Phase 1 (Foundation Build):** 59 additional pages across a 4–6 week sprint. Adds all 28 service cluster pages, Service Hub infrastructure, 26 location pages, 5 blog posts, and E-E-A-T support pages. Reaches the 80-page milestone from the original plan.
**Phase 2 (Monthly Rollout):** 10 pages/month for 12 months. Unchanged from the v2.1 plan. Completes remaining service clusters, fills the geographic footprint, adds trust pages and blog content driven by performance data. Target: ~200 pages at 12 months.
The companion Navigation & Mega Menu Creative Brief details how the nav system evolves across these three phases.

The current site has roughly 30–35 indexed URLs. The new architecture consolidates that content into 21 rich pages at Phase 0 launch. Six separate gallery pages become one filterable Portfolio page. | **Phase** | **Name** | **Pages** | **Scope** | **Nav State** |

| Phase 2 | Monthly Rollout | +10/month | Remaining clusters, Tier 3–4 towns, trust pages, flex content. | Items added via CMS toggle as pages publish. |

## **3.1 Strategic Rationale**
Phase 0 gets the new brand, design system, and five-pillar architecture in front of visitors and search engines as fast as possible. The pillar pages are 3,000–3,500+ word comprehensive hubs that outperform the current site’s thin pages from Day 1.
Phase 1 builds the depth layer that drives rankings and AI citations. Twenty-nine cluster pages create the long-tail targeting surface. Twenty-six location pages establish geographic authority across five counties. The Service Hub centralizes trust content (warranties, FAQs, care instructions) that both pre-sale and post-sale visitors need.
Phase 2 fills remaining gaps using performance data. Google Search Console reveals which clusters gain traction, which locations attract demand, and where blog content can reinforce winning positions.

## **4.1 What Phase 0 Does NOT Include**
**Deferred to Phase 1:** All 28 service cluster pages. Service Hub + 3 children (Warranties, FAQs, Care Instructions). All 5 county pages. All 21 town pages (Tier 1 + Tier 2). All 5 Service + Location hybrid pages. All 5 blog posts. All E-E-A-T support pages (author bios + Editorial Standards).
**Deferred to Phase 2:** Trust pages (Design Technology, Awards, Insurance & Licensing). Remaining 26 cluster pages. Tier 3–4 towns (32 pages). Additional blog posts and flex content.
## **4.2 Phase 0 Schema Requirements**
Even at Quick Launch, these schema types must be implemented:
**LocalBusiness:** Homepage, About Us, Contact Us, Service Areas.
**Service:** All 5 pillar pages.
**Organization:** About Us, Our Story. Includes sameAs links to GBP, social profiles, BBB.
**BreadcrumbList:** All pages (sitewide).
**ImageGallery:** Portfolio pages.
**AggregateRating:** Reviews page (Trustindex widget or dynamic schema).
**NOTE:** FAQ schema, Article schema, Person schema, HowTo schema, and speakable are not needed at Phase 0 because their host pages (cluster pages, blog posts, author bios) do not exist yet.
