## Critical Architectural Decisions

### The Advisory Pivot (April 2026)
Lisa discontinued Full Response Management (FRM), the highest-priced outsourced service tier. The company repositioned entirely as a **Win Strategist** advisory firm.

**Core positioning:** *"Your team writes it. We make it win."*

**Three-path architecture replaces four-path:**
1. **Win Strategist** ($10M–$25M+ revenue) — Primary revenue driver
2. **Guided System / Express** ($1M–$5M revenue)
3. **DIY Resources** ($500K–$1M revenue)

### Service Renaming (Win Strategist Path)
- "Proposal Audit" → **Evaluator's Eye Audit**
- "Strategic & Compliance Review" → **Win Strategy Review**
- "Content Refinement" → **Embedded Content Refinement** (flagship offering)
- **Play to Win Consults** — unchanged
- **Library Content Development** — unchanged

### Universal Brand Rules
- The RFP Success® Company — ® symbol on every public-facing brand mention
- "RFP-expert Lisa Rehurek" — required prefix on early-page Lisa mentions (per Master Feedback Tracker §2.2)
- 30+ years of RFP experience (Lisa, not the company)
- 76%+ win rate (not 78%)
- No federal market positioning anywhere
- "Service-based SLED" or "service-based businesses" — not just "businesses"

## Reference Hierarchy When Rules Conflict

1. **Sound like Lisa Rehurek.** Brand voice wins over every structural requirement. If an SEO rule makes content sound machine-written, break the SEO rule and sound like Lisa.
2. **Add information gain.** Every page must contain knowledge competing pages don't have.
3. **Tell stories with real humans.** Named clients, evaluator perspectives, specific proposal outcomes.
4. **Get entity SEO right.** Brand name density, Lisa attribution, schema, Win Strategist positioning.
5. **Get technical SEO right.** Keyword placement, linking, metadata, AEO/GEO capsules.
