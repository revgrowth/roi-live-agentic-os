## The Strategic Pivot (Critical Context)

In April 2026, Lisa Rehurek discontinued Full Response Management (FRM), her highest-priced service tier. The company repositioned as a **Win Strategist** advisory firm with the core line *"Your team writes it. We make it win."*

All documents have been swept of FRM-specific copy, federal language (no federal market positioning), 26-year references (now 30+), and 78% win rate (now 76%+). The homepage went from four paths to three. The Advisement path was elevated to primary revenue driver. See `02_memory/USER_MEMORY.md` for the full set of corrections that must hold going forward.

## Five Customer Archetypes (Used Throughout)

Content must speak to one or more of these:

1. **The Scrambler** — Reactive, deadline-driven, no defined process
2. **The Almost-Winner** — Strong shortlist rate, low conversion, doesn't know why
3. **The Capability Dumper** — Writes about themselves instead of the buyer
4. **The First-Timer** — Pursuing first SLED contract, doesn't know what they don't know
5. **The Internal Champion** — Has buy-in but needs ammunition to defend the spend
