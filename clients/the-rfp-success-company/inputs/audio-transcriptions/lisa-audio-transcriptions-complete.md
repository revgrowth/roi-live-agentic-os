## Audio File 2: Advisement Services.m4a
**Duration:** ~12 minutes
**Topic:** The Five Advisement Service Offerings
### Key Content Summary:
This audio provides detailed information about the advisement services tier, designed for businesses that have existing teams but need expert guidance.
**Target Market:**
- Service-based businesses selling to state and local governments (SLED)
- $2M-$10M revenue range
- Bidding on contracts of $150,000 and up
- Currently responding to RFPs but not achieving consistent results
**Core Problem Addressed:**
Teams are qualifying and putting in effort, but have low or inconsistent win rates. Their proposals are adequate but not compelling enough to win against competitors.
**Overall Positioning:**
- Provides expert evaluator's eye, strategic clarity, and honest feedback
- Addition to existing team (not a takeover)
- For high-performing teams looking to "win more" or "upgrade and elevate"
- Guided by principle: "You can't see the picture when you're inside the frame"
### The Five Advisement Services:
#### 1. Proposal Audit
**For:** Businesses asking "What are we doing wrong? Why aren't we winning?"
**Process:**
- Reviews three past proposals
- Evaluates for compliance, positioning, clarity, differentiation, and risk
- Provides in-document comments on key areas for change
- Delivers strategic report or checklist
- Includes practical writing guide
- Concludes with live call to walk through findings
**Value:** Fastest way to uncover blind spots and gain objective clarity. Often leads to other services.
#### 2. Content Refinement
**For:** Teams working on "must-win" proposals who need writing enhancement
**Process:**
- Refines key sections tied to evaluation criteria
- Improves positioning and messaging
- Includes compliance review
- Actively edits and rewrites sections (vs. just advising)
**Value:** Makes proposals sharper, clearer, more evaluator-aligned. Shifts from "capabilities dump" to strategic positioning.
**Distinction:** Content Refinement = editing/rewriting; Strategic Review = telling what to do
#### 3. Strategic Review
**For:** Businesses writing their own proposals but needing expert strategic input
**Process:**
- Helps define bid strategy on front end
- Reviews proposal on back end
- Advises on what needs to be fixed
- Provides strategic direction from external perspective
**Value:** Ensures strategic soundness while client retains full ownership of writing.
**Distinction:** Strategic Review = telling what to do; Content Refinement = doing the editing
#### 4. Play to Win Consults
**For:** Organizations seeking strategic foundation for bidding process
**Questions Addressed:**
- What RFPs should we actively pursue?
- How do we differentiate ourselves?
- What should we be bidding on?
- How do we build a repeatable approach to winning?
**Process:**
- Live, 2-3 hour strategy session
- Deep dive into decision-making process
- Identify what to bid on and what not to bid on
- Identify differentiators, competitors, competitive advantages
- Develop value propositions at business development level (not individual RFP level)
**Value:** Provides clarity, alignment, strategic lens. Moves from chasing everything to strategic alignment.
#### 5. Library Content Development
**For:** Teams tired of "reinventing the wheel" or who believe current library content isn't strong enough
**Process:**
- Revises existing library content or creates new content for standard proposal sections
- Tailors content to client's brand, services, and differentiators
- Client owns content for future use and customization
**Value:** Eliminates starting from scratch, ensures powerful content, provides objective review.
### Overall Goal:
Shift clients from reactive state ("I don't know what we're doing wrong") to proactive state ("I understand why this will score well").
**Common Objections:**
- Internal capability questions
- Time requirements
- Lack of guaranteed wins
**Engagement Model:**
- Can be premier client or downsell from Full Response Management
- Supports small teams without full outsourcing
- Services can be standalone or lead to ongoing relationships
