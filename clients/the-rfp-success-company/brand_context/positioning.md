## Proprietary frameworks

**4x4 Framework™** — the proprietary methodology. Four pillars, each with four sub-components:

1. **Content** — D.A.R.E. lives here (Distinction, Answer the Questions, Readability, Evidence & Authority)
2. **Strategy** — foundation, positioning, mindset, beyond-the-document intelligence
3. **People** — right roles, teamwork, specialized seats, management cadence
4. **Process** — Go/No-Go selectivity, planning, repeatable workflows, review rigor

**D.A.R.E. Method** — the tactical writing methodology inside the Content pillar:

- **D**istinction — what sets you apart (not baseline qualifications)
- **A**nswer the Questions — direct, complete responses; core mantra "IT IS NOT ABOUT YOU"
- **R**eadability — short sentences, target 6th-grade reading level (8 words = 100% comprehension; 43 words = <10%)
- **E**vidence & Authority — proof, stats, references, certifications, thought leadership

Older versions of the D.A.R.E. eBook used "Requirements" for R; the 2026 version uses "Readability." The eBook PDF needs updating before launch.

**STAND OUT** — Lisa's proposal communication model (Share Successes / Them-Focused / Attention Grabbing / Narrow Down / Duplication / Optical / Understand styles / Tell stories).

## Vertical and entity scope

**In scope:** State, Local, Education (SLED). Specific industries with case study evidence: Technology/IT (primary), Education, Transportation/Fleet, Travel Management, Government Web Development/GovTech, Disaster Recovery, Security Services, Specialty Services, NEMT.

**Adjacent / handle carefully:** Healthcare and Healthcare IT — case study evidence exists (Optumas, Medecision, Edlaw, Lodestar) but v2.2 explicitly notes "Healthcare IT is NOT a strength." Resolve with Lisa before publishing industry pages claiming healthcare strength.

**Out of scope:** Federal contracts (FAR-regulated, different timeline rhythm), Commercial RFPs (less regulated, different format), individual federal subcontracting work.

## What Lisa explicitly will not say or sell

- Guarantees of winning
- Hourly billing (always per-project, complexity-based)
- Done-for-you proposal writing in the FRM sense (discontinued April 2026)
- "We'll just slap something together" — meaningful timelines are non-negotiable
- Industry-specific writers as the value prop (the value is proposal management + persuasive writing; the client's SMEs bring industry knowledge)
