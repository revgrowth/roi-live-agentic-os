## Sales call language (from Lisa's audio)

For FAQ content, sales enablement, objection handling, onboarding:

| Question | How Lisa handles it |
|---|---|
| "How do your services work, start to finish?" | Walks through engagement model; emphasizes what we handle vs. what they handle |
| "How does pricing work? Is it hourly?" | Per-project fee based on RFP complexity. "You're gonna know upfront what it costs." |
| "Can you do this in a couple of days?" | Education moment: walks through why a quality response requires the full timeline |
| "Do you have writers who specialize in our industry?" | "Our expertise comes in the project management aspect... and the writing aspect. The technical information is YOUR company information." |
| "Got a lower quote elsewhere" | Cheaper vendors lack PM, strategy, review, compliance — may over-use AI |
| "Not sure the ROI is there" | Cost of a mediocre proposal vs. a single win paying for years of investment |
