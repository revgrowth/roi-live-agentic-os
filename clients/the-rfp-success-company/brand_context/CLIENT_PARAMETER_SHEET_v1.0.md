### **4.6 Industry-Specific Language Notes**

Technical terms and phrasing specific to RFP consulting and SLED government contracting:

- SLED (State, Local, Education) — always spelled out on first mention, abbreviated thereafter

- RFP (Request for Proposal) — commonly understood, spell out once on first page mention

- GWAC, IDIQ, BPA — federal-ecosystem terms; avoid on primary SLED content unless the SLED vs. Federal comparison specifically calls for them

- "Evaluator" preferred over "reviewer" or "procurement officer" — reflects scoring role

- "Proposal" (not "pitch," not "bid" — though "bid" acceptable in capture/go-no-go context)

- "Contract value" (not "deal size")

- "Down-select rate" and "finalist rate" — industry terminology for advancing in evaluation rounds
