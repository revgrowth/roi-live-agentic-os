# Advisement Services - Complete Service Guide
## Overview
**Target Market:** Service-based businesses selling to state and local governments (SLED) in the $2M-$10M revenue range, bidding on contracts of $150,000 and up.
**Core Problem Addressed:** Businesses are responding to RFPs, qualifying, and putting in effort, but not achieving consistent or desired results. They have low or inconsistent win rates. While they believe their proposals are adequate, they are not compelling enough to win against competitors.
**Positioning:** Advisement services provide an expert evaluator's eye, strategic clarity, and honest feedback. These services act as an addition to the client's existing team rather than a full takeover. They're designed for high-performing teams looking to "win more" or "upgrade and elevate" their capabilities, not just for struggling businesses.
**Value Proposition:** Help clients shift from a reactive state of "I don't know what we're doing wrong" to a proactive state of "I understand why this will score well." The goal is to improve proposal quality, increase scores, and move clients towards more strategic decision-making and better positioning with evaluators.
**Key Principle:** "You can't see the picture when you're inside the frame" - emphasizing the value of an external, objective perspective.

## The Five Advisement Service Offerings
### 1. Evaluator's Eye Audit
**What It Is:**
An in-depth review of past proposals to identify areas for improvement and uncover blind spots.
**Who It's For:**
Businesses actively asking:
- "What are we doing wrong?"
- "Why aren't we winning?"
- "Why aren't we winning more?"
**How It Works:**
- Reviews three past proposals
- Evaluates proposals for compliance, positioning, clarity, differentiation, and risk
- Provides in-document comments on key areas for change
- Delivers a strategic report or checklist summarizing key items that, if changed, will elevate proposals
- Includes a copy of the "practical writing guide"
- Concludes with a live call to walk through findings and answer questions
**Problems It Solves:**
- Uncovers blind spots
- Provides objective clarity
- Offers a roadmap for improvement
- Helps clients realize they may be losing not due to being unqualified, but because their value isn't being translated effectively or they aren't answering questions appropriately
**Deliverables:**
- In-document comments on three past proposals
- Strategic report or checklist
- Practical writing guide
- Live call with the team to review findings
**Positioning:**
Often the fastest way to uncover blind spots and gain objective clarity. Can serve as a pathway to other services.
**Engagement Type:**
Typically a "one-and-done" service, though audits tend to lead to other services more frequently than content refinement.
---
### 2. Embedded Content Refinement
**What It Is:**
Strengthening the writing of specific, crucial pieces of a proposal through professional editing and rewriting.
**Who It's For:**
Teams working on a "must-win" proposal who need help enhancing their writing.
**How It Works:**
- Refines key sections of the writing, generally tied to evaluation criteria
- Focuses on improving positioning and messaging
- Includes a compliance review to ensure alignment
- Actively edits and rewrites specific sections (vs. just telling clients what to do)
**Problems It Solves:**
- Makes proposals sharper, clearer, and more aligned with evaluator needs
- Shifts proposals from being a "capabilities dump" to sounding more strategic
- Provides professional-level proposal language without outsourcing the entire response
**Deliverables:**
- Refined proposal sections
- Improved positioning and messaging
- Compliance review
**Positioning:**
Described as "editing" to elevate the writing. Clients receive professional-level proposal language without outsourcing the entire response.
**Key Distinction:**
Embedded Content Refinement involves actively editing and rewriting specific sections, whereas Win Strategy Review involves telling clients what to do.
**Engagement Type:**
Often a "one-and-done" service, similar to the audit, but audit tends to lead to other services more frequently.
---
### 3. Win Strategy Review
**What It Is:**
Providing high-level strategic direction on proposal development without doing the actual writing.
**Who It's For:**
Businesses that are writing and owning their proposals but need expert strategic input.
**How It Works:**
- Helps define the bid strategy on the front end
- Reviews the proposal on the back end
- Advises on what needs to be fixed
- Provides strategic direction from someone who has not been "in the weeds"
**Problems It Solves:**
Ensures the proposal is strategically sound from the outset and provides guidance for necessary corrections.
**Deliverables:**
- Strategic direction and recommendations
- Front-end bid strategy guidance
- Back-end proposal review with fix recommendations
**Key Distinction:**
Win Strategy Review involves telling clients *what* to do, whereas Embedded Content Refinement involves actively *editing and rewriting* specific sections.
**Positioning:**
Clients retain full ownership of the writing while receiving high-level strategic guidance from an objective expert perspective.
---
### 4. Play to Win Consults
**What It Is:**
A comprehensive strategic foundation session focused on building a repeatable approach to winning, rather than just tactical fixes.
**Who It's For:**
Organizations seeking a strategic foundation for their bidding process and asking questions like:
- What RFPs should we actively pursue?
- How do we differentiate ourselves?
- What should we be bidding on?
- How do we build a repeatable approach to winning?
**How It Works:**
- Live, 2-3 hour strategy session
- Dives deep into the organization's decision-making process
- Helps identify what to bid on and what not to bid on
- Assists in identifying differentiators, competitors, competitive advantages, and value propositions at a business development level (not an individual RFP level)
**Problems It Solves:**
- Provides clarity, alignment, and a strategic lens for future pursuits
- Helps clients move from chasing everything to aligning with opportunities that fit their strategy
- Builds a repeatable approach to winning
**Deliverables:**
- Strategic insights on bid selection
- Differentiation strategy
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Value proposition development
- Business development-level strategic framework
**Positioning:**
Focuses on building a repeatable approach to winning and strategic alignment for long-term success. This is about foundational strategy, not individual RFP tactics.
**Session Format:**
Live, 2-3 hour intensive strategy session
---
### 5. Library Content Development
**What It Is:**
Creating or revising a library of reusable content for standard proposal sections.
**Who It's For:**
Teams that are:
- Tired of "reinventing the wheel" for each proposal
- Believe their current library content is not strong enough
- Want an objective review of their existing content
**How It Works:**
- Revises existing library content or creates fully written content for standard proposal sections
- Content is tailored to the client's brand, services, and differentiators
- The developed content is owned by the client for future use and customization in individual RFPs
**Problems It Solves:**
- Eliminates the need to start from scratch on every proposal
- Ensures existing content is powerful and effective
- Provides objective review of current content
**Deliverables:**
- Revised or newly created library content for standard proposal sections
- Content tailored to client's brand, services, and differentiators
- Reusable, client-owned content for future customization
**Positioning:**
Empowers teams to efficiently create customized proposals by leveraging a strong, pre-developed content library. Guided by the principle: "You can't see the picture when you're inside the frame."
**Note:**
This service is also included as part of the Full Response Management Partnership, where a content library is built during the engagement process.

## Overall Service Philosophy
**Not About Replacement:**
These services are not about replacing the client's team but about supporting and elevating them.
**For High Performers Too:**
Advisement is positioned as a service for high-performing teams looking to "win more" or "upgrade and elevate" their capabilities, not just for struggling businesses.
**Evaluator-Aligned:**
All services focus on understanding how evaluators think and score, ensuring proposals are positioned to maximize scores.
**Strategic Shift:**
Moving clients from reactive ("What are we doing wrong?") to proactive ("I understand why this will score well").

## Integration with Other Offerings
**Relationship to Full Response Management:**
Advisement services can serve as an entry point or alternative to Full Response Management for clients who want to maintain ownership of their proposal process.
**Relationship to RFP Success Express:**
For clients not ready for advisement services or Full Response Management, RFP Success Express (the SaaS platform) provides frameworks and insights at a more accessible price point.
**Service Pathway:**
Many clients start with an audit or Play to Win Consult, then either:
- Move to ongoing advisement services
- Upgrade to Full Response Management
- Use insights to improve their internal process
