## **TEMPLATE OVERVIEW**
This template serves one page at launch: Lisa Rehurek's bio at \`/lisa-rehurek/\`. Per Core Standards Phase 4.7, every named expert on a client site requires a dedicated bio page at a stable URL. Lisa is the site's only named expert.
The page does three distinct jobs simultaneously:
1. **Establishes Lisa as the recognized subject-matter expert in SLED RFP consulting.** This is an E-E-A-T Expertise signal (Phase 4.2). It's also a Knowledge Panel seed per Phase 11.6 — a prerequisite for Lisa appearing in Google Knowledge Panels and AI system answer cards.
2. **Serves as the Person entity declaration** (Phase 12.2) for the entire site. Every page that references Lisa via byline, attribution, or quote references this page's Person schema via \`@id\`. Getting the schema right once here means every other page on the site inherits correct entity declaration.
3. **Converts pre-qualified visitors.** Visitors arriving on \`/lisa-rehurek/\` are almost always deep in the buying journey — they've already read at least one service page or case study, and they're trying to answer "is this person actually credible?" The page must turn that verification into a conversion moment.
No other page on the site combines these three jobs. The About page (T10) is about the company. The service pages (T3) are about the engagement. The case studies (T4) are about the outcomes. Only T9 is about the person behind it all.

# **PART 1 — TEMPLATE STRATEGY**
## **1.1 Template Purpose**
The author bio page is the canonical entity page for a named expert. When a visitor clicks "Lisa Rehurek" anywhere on the site, they arrive here. When an AI answer engine (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overview) tries to identify "who is Lisa Rehurek?" — it crawls this page. When Google evaluates the site's E-E-A-T signals and searches for the author's credentials behind every byline — it reads this page.
Three specific jobs:
**First, it proves the expert exists as a real, credentialed person.** This is a Trust signal (Phase 4.4). Real photo, specific career history, verifiable credentials, documented publications. Not marketing fluff — documented facts that can be cross-referenced against Amazon author pages, LinkedIn, press mentions, and public records.
**Second, it establishes topical authority.** The \`knowsAbout\` array in Person schema tells AI systems and search engines what this person is credible about. Ten topical areas on this page (per Parameter Sheet §3.3): SLED RFP Consulting, Government Proposal Strategy, RFP Response Management, Proposal Positioning & Differentiation, Proposal Evaluation & Scoring Psychology, State Government Procurement, Local Government Contracting, Education Procurement, Proposal Capture Management, Proposal Audit & Compliance Review.
**Third, it feeds Knowledge Panel eligibility.** Per Core Standards Phase 11.6, Knowledge Panels require multiple signals: Wikipedia, Wikidata, consistent sameAs entity declarations, and a canonical author entity page on the subject's primary domain. This page is that canonical declaration. Neither Lisa's brand nor Lisa herself currently has a Google Knowledge Panel (per Parameter Sheet §9.6) — building this page with proper schema is step one of a multi-step Knowledge Panel seeding strategy.
**Strategic context:** This page is referenced by approximately 60+ pages at launch (every page with a Lisa byline or attribution, every service page, every case study, every blog post's author byline once the content roadmap fires). Every time another page ships on the site, its schema references this page's Person \`@id\`. Getting this page right now pays compounding returns for the entire 165-page site.

## **1.4 Target Audience**
Three reader types arrive at this page:
### **Audience A: The Verifier**
Came from a service page or case study. Mid-funnel. Wants to confirm Lisa is real, credentialed, and not overclaiming. Scans the page in 30 seconds looking for:
*   Professional headshot (real person)
*   Specific years of experience (30+)
*   External credentials (Inc. 5000, WBENC, Goldman Sachs badges)
*   Documented published work (Amazon link confirms actual books)
*   LinkedIn link (confirms the professional identity)
Once verified, routes back to the service page or to booking a discovery call.
### **Audience B: The Researcher**
Came from Google, AI answer engine, podcast show notes, or a LinkedIn click. Top-funnel. Asking "who is this person?" before deciding whether to engage with the brand at all. Reads the bio prose, scans the credentials wall, and evaluates whether Lisa is a credible subject-matter expert in government RFP consulting.
This reader spends 2–3 minutes on the page. They may or may not click through to services. The conversion goal for this reader is building awareness and topical association — "Lisa Rehurek = RFP expert" — that pays off on a later visit.
### **Audience C: The Journalist / Podcaster / Partner**
Came looking for a speaker, interview subject, expert source, or partnership opportunity. Wants to find Lisa's contact info, confirmed credentials, topics of expertise, and prior media appearances quickly.
Conversion goal: contact form submission or direct email via the Contact section on the page.
## **1.5 Conversion Psychology**
Unlike a service page that closes by making a pitch, the author bio page closes by demonstrating that there's no pitch needed. The page's psychological move is: "Here are the facts. Draw your own conclusion."
Three principles:
1. **Show, don't tell.** Credentials appear as badges with verification links, not as prose claims. Book titles appear with Amazon links. Publications appear with publication names. Awards appear with award-year and issuing-body attribution. Every claim links to external verification.
2. **Voice over resume.** The 200–400 word bio isn't a resume timeline. It's Lisa telling her story in her own voice (per Brand Voice v2.2 §2). Why she cares about government RFPs. What she learned from her first loss. How the 4x4 Framework™ emerged from lived experience. The resume data lives in the credentials wall; the bio prose lives in the voice.
3. **Authority without ego.** Per Brand Voice v2.2, Lisa's DNA is "confident but not arrogant — leads with credibility, not ego." The page must project authority through accumulated evidence (publications, awards, client results) rather than self-praise. The subject of most sentences is the work, the methodology, or the client — not Lisa.

# **PART 4 — TEMPLATE VARIATION FOR FUTURE AUTHOR PAGES**
T9 serves one page at launch (\`/lisa-rehurek/\`). This section documents the rules for adding additional author bio pages as the team scales — reserved for future growth.
## **4.1 When to Add a New T9 Page**
A new T9 author bio page is required when ALL of the following are true:
*   The new expert is named publicly on the site (byline, quoted, credited in methodology)
*   The expert has documented credentials (years of experience, named role, verifiable external recognition)
*   The expert's work will appear across multiple pages (not a one-time guest author)
*   The expert has at least one external sameAs reference (LinkedIn minimum)
Bench contractors and non-public-facing team members do NOT require T9 pages (per Parameter Sheet §3.4). Susan and Anna, who contribute to Brand Voice intelligence but are not public-facing, are documented in internal records only.
## **4.2 URL Pattern for Additional Author Pages**
Follow the Lisa pattern: \`/\[firstname-lastname\]/\`
Examples:
*   \`/lisa-rehurek/\` (existing)
*   \`/\[new-author-name\]/\` (pattern)
Alternative pattern (if \`/team/\` hub is built): \`/team/\[firstname-lastname\]/\`
## **4.3 Schema Adjustments for Secondary Experts**
For non-founder experts, the schema differs from Lisa's:
*   Person schema still required, but NOT marked as the Organization's \`founder\`
*   \`worksFor\` still references Organization
*   \`@id\` pattern: \`https://therfpsuccesscompany.com/#\[firstname-lastname\]\`
*   Does not override the canonical Person entity (Lisa remains the primary expert for the organization)
## **4.4 Design Variations for Team Page Integration**
If a \`/team/\` hub is added with multiple expert bios:
*   Hub page uses T8 Index/Hub template, displaying each expert as a card
*   Individual expert pages use T9 with design inheriting from Lisa's page
*   Lisa's page retains premium visual treatment (founder emphasis)
*   Secondary experts use standard T9 treatment without founder-specific elements
## **4.5 Content Scope Reduction for Secondary Experts**
Secondary expert bios may compress certain sections:

| **Section** | **Lisa (Founder)** | **Secondary Expert** |
| ---| ---| --- |
| Bio prose | 300–450 words | 200–300 words |
| Credentials wall | Full (9+ awards) | Adjusted to expert's recognition |
| Published work | 11 publications | Whatever applies |
| Podcast archive | Full host treatment | Omitted unless relevant |
| Methodology attribution | 4x4 + D.A.R.E. | Omitted (methodologies remain Lisa's attribution) |
| Services Led | All 5 Win Strategist services | Specific services the expert leads |
| Client Results | Full portfolio | Engagements the expert directly led |
