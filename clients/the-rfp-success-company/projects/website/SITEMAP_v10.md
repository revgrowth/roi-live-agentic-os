### Embedded Content Refinement ($12K–$18K) — Flagship Win Strategist Offering

- We work alongside your team to strengthen draft content so each section is clear, persuasive, and positioned to win. This is not editing. This is strategic shaping.
- Client retains PM, timelines, SME coordination, final assembly
- Includes: Kickoff Bid Strategy Session, review of client-drafted content, rewrite key sections, strategic positioning, differentiation
- Focus on highest-scoring sections with light compliance awareness
- Does NOT include: Project management, SME interviewing, full-document writing, submittal ownership
- Positioned as the premium advisory offering for must-win proposals
- Page treatment: T3 Individual Service Page with premium visual modifier (badge, border, or background shade per Page Template Map §6)

### Win Strategy Review ($7.5K–$10K) — Champagne Detail

- Acts as evaluators, strategists, and compliance watchdogs
- Client retains responsibility for fixes
- Includes: Strategic review of full proposal (scoring weaknesses, missing win themes, evaluator misalignment, structural issues), formal compliance review with written findings and prioritized recommendations
- Does NOT include: Rewriting, PM, SME work, content creation
- Positioned as executive-level advisory review and compliance risk mitigation
