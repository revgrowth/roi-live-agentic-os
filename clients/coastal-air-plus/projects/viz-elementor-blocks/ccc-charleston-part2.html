Yes — significantly. Properties east of the Ashley River, on James Island, and within two miles of the harbor experience accelerated corrosion on copper refrigerant lines from salt air infiltration. This makes refrigerant leaks more common in coastal Charleston than in inland markets like Columbia or Greenville. Outdoor condenser coils also accumulate salt deposits that reduce heat transfer efficiency over time. Systems in these zones benefit from annual coil cleaning and more frequent refrigerant pressure checks than the manufacturer's standard maintenance schedule recommends.

Short cycling is usually caused by an oversized system, a failing capacitor, low refrigerant triggering safety shutoffs, or a thermostat malfunction. In newer Charleston developments, builder-grade systems are often oversized for the actual cooling load, which causes short cycles and inadequate dehumidification — the home reaches the set temperature but still feels clammy because the system never runs long enough to properly remove moisture.
